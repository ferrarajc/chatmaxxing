@section glossary | Glossary | Definitions of the financial, regulatory, and internal terms used throughout the library, A to Z.

@page glossary-a-d | Glossary A–D
@summary Definitions of terms beginning A through D.

## A

### ACAT
Automated Customer Account Transfer. The industry system for moving brokerage accounts between firms, started by the receiving firm. See [[acat-incoming]].

### ACH
Automated Clearing House. The U.S. electronic bank transfer network; free, 2–3 business days. See [[ach-transfers]].

### ACW (after-chat work)
The wrap-up code, summary, and coaching generated when a chat ends, reviewed and submitted by the agent. See [[after-chat-work]].

### Agent (under a POA)
The person authorized to act for another under a power of attorney. See [[power-of-attorney]].

### AML
Anti-money-laundering. Laws and programs to detect and report suspicious financial activity. See [[aml-red-flags]].

### Annualized return
The average yearly return over a multi-year period. See [[performance-reporting]].

### Asset allocation
The division of investments among stocks, bonds, and cash. See [[asset-allocation]].

### Authorized user
A person given view-only, limited, or full access to a client's account by the owner. See [[authorized-users-access-levels]].

### Autopilot
The agent-workspace feature that sends AI-drafted messages on a timer, within a scope. See [[autopilot-overview]].

### Average cost
The default cost basis method: the average price of all shares owned. See [[cost-basis-methods]].

## B

### Backdoor Roth
A nondeductible Traditional IRA contribution followed by a Roth conversion. See [[backdoor-roth]].

### Backup withholding
24% withholding required when a client's TIN is missing or incorrect. See [[w9-backup-withholding]].

### Basis (cost basis)
What was paid for shares, used to calculate gains and losses. For IRAs, basis means nondeductible contributions.

### Beneficiary
A person or entity designated to receive an account at the owner's death. See [[beneficiaries-overview]].

### Benchmark
The index a fund tracks or is measured against. See [[benchmarks-explained]].

### Beta
A measure of how much a fund has moved relative to the S&P 500.

### Bond
A loan to a government or company that pays interest. See [[bonds-and-interest-rates]].

## C

### Capital gain distribution
A fund's payout of net realized gains, usually in December. See [[capital-gains-distributions]].

### Catch-up contribution
The additional IRA contribution allowed at age 50+ ($1,100 for 2026). See [[catch-up-contributions]].

### CIP
Customer Identification Program; identity verification for new accounts. See [[cip-identity-verification]].

### Contingent beneficiary
A secondary beneficiary who inherits if all primary beneficiaries have died. See [[primary-contingent]].

### Conversion (Roth)
Moving pre-tax IRA money to a Roth IRA, a taxable event. See [[roth-conversion-overview]].

### Covered shares
Shares acquired since 2012 whose basis we report to the IRS. See [[covered-noncovered-shares]].

### Custodial account
An account held for a minor under UTMA or UGMA. See [[custodial-accounts]].

### Cutoff
4:00 PM ET; orders received before it get that day's NAV. See [[trade-cutoff-nav]].

## D

### Designated beneficiary
An individual named as an IRA beneficiary; most are subject to the 10-year rule. See [[non-spouse-beneficiaries]].

### Direct rollover
A rollover paid directly from a plan to an IRA. See [[direct-rollovers]].

### Distribution (fund)
A payment of dividends or capital gains by a fund. See [[distributions-overview]].

### Distribution (IRA)
Money taken out of an IRA. See [[ira-distributions-overview]].

### Diversification
Spreading investments to reduce the impact of any single holding. See [[diversification]].

### Dollar-cost averaging
Investing a fixed amount at regular intervals. See [[dollar-cost-averaging]].

### DRIP
Dividend reinvestment; distributions buy more shares automatically. See [[drip]].

### Duration
A bond fund's sensitivity to interest-rate changes, in years. See [[duration]].

@page glossary-e-l | Glossary E–L
@summary Definitions of terms beginning E through L.

## E

### Early withdrawal penalty
The 10% additional tax on IRA distributions before 59½, unless an exception applies. See [[early-withdrawal-penalty]].

### Eligible designated beneficiary (EDB)
A beneficiary who may stretch inherited IRA payments over life expectancy. See [[eligible-designated-beneficiaries]].

### ESG
Environmental, social, and governance criteria used to screen investments; BFESG applies such screens.

### Escheatment
Turning dormant property over to a state. See [[dormant-accounts-escheatment]].

### Evidence highlighting
Transcript highlights showing where each Proposed Action value came from. See [[evidence-highlighting]].

### Excess contribution
An IRA contribution above the allowed amount, subject to 6% annual excise tax. See [[excess-contributions]].

### Exchange
Moving money from one fund to another in the same account. See [[exchanges-overview]].

### Ex-dividend date
The date a fund's NAV drops by a distribution. See [[ex-dividend-dates]].

### Expense ratio
A fund's annual operating cost as a percentage of assets. See [[expense-ratios]].

## F

### FIFO
First in, first out; a cost basis method selling the oldest shares first.

### FINRA
The Financial Industry Regulatory Authority, which regulates broker-dealers.

### Form 1099-B, 1099-DIV, 1099-R, 5498
Tax forms we issue. See [[tax-center-overview]].

### Frequent trading policy
Restricts purchases after a round trip within 30 days. See [[frequent-trading-policy]].

### Full redemption
Selling all shares of a fund in an account. See [[partial-full-redemptions]].

## G–H

### Good order
An order with all information needed to process it.

### Growth (style)
Companies with above-average expected earnings growth. See [[fund-groups-explained]].

### Holding period
How long shares were held; over one year is long-term.

## I

### In kind
Moving securities as-is rather than selling for cash.

### Index fund
A fund that tracks an index. See [[index-vs-active]].

### Indirect rollover
A 60-day rollover in which the client receives the money first. See [[indirect-rollovers]].

### Inherited IRA
An IRA held for a beneficiary after the owner's death. See [[inherited-ira]].

### Intent label / intent summary
The AI-generated description of why a client is chatting, shown at escalation. See [[incoming-contacts]].

### Interested party
A person who receives duplicate statements. See [[duplicate-statements]].

## J–L

### JTWROS
Joint tenants with rights of survivorship; the survivor inherits automatically. See [[joint-accounts]].

### Letters Testamentary / of Administration
Court documents authorizing an executor or administrator. See [[estate-documentation]].

### Limited access
An authorized-user level allowing trades and payments back to the owner only.

### Linked bank account
A verified bank account used for electronic transfers. See [[linked-bank-accounts]].

### Long-term capital gain
A gain on shares held more than a year, taxed at lower rates. See [[capital-gains-rates]].

@page glossary-m-r | Glossary M–R
@summary Definitions of terms beginning M through R.

## M

### MAGI
Modified adjusted gross income; used for Roth eligibility and deductibility.

### Medallion signature guarantee
A stamp from a financial institution guaranteeing a signature. See [[medallion-signature-guarantee]].

### Micro-deposits
Two small deposits used to verify a bank account. See [[bank-linking-verification]].

### Mutual fund
A pooled investment owned by its shareholders. See [[mutual-fund-basics]].

## N

### NAV
Net asset value per share; a mutual fund's daily price. See [[nav-pricing]].

### Net investment income tax
A 3.8% tax on investment income above certain income levels.

### Noncovered shares
Shares whose cost basis we don't report to the IRS. See [[covered-noncovered-shares]].

## O–P

### OFAC
The Treasury office that administers sanctions. See [[ofac-sanctions]].

### Paperless
Electronic delivery of documents. See [[paperless-delivery]].

### Per stirpes
A designation passing a deceased beneficiary's share to their descendants. See [[per-stirpes]].

### POA
Power of attorney. See [[power-of-attorney]].

### Primary beneficiary
A beneficiary who inherits first. See [[primary-contingent]].

### Pro-rata rule
Conversions are taxed in proportion to pre-tax and after-tax money across all IRAs. See [[conversion-taxes]].

### Proposed Action
The structured card a task expert prepares for agent approval. See [[proposed-action-card]].

### Prospectus
A fund's legal disclosure document. See [[prospectus]].

## Q

### QCD
Qualified charitable distribution from an IRA at 70½+. See [[qualified-charitable-distributions]].

### QDRO
Qualified domestic relations order dividing an employer plan in divorce. See [[qdro-vs-divorce-decree]].

### Qualified dividend
A dividend taxed at long-term capital gain rates. See [[qualified-dividends]].

### Qualified distribution (Roth)
A tax-free Roth withdrawal meeting the age and five-year tests. See [[roth-distribution-rules]].

## R

### Recharacterization
Treating a contribution as made to the other IRA type. See [[recharacterizations]].

### Record date
The date that determines who receives a distribution.

### Redemption
Selling fund shares. See [[redemptions-overview]].

### Redemption hold
A hold on proceeds from recently purchased shares until payment clears. See [[redemption-holds]].

### Reference number
The `REF-XXXXXX` identifier for actions submitted in chat. See [[reference-numbers]].

### Required beginning date
April 1 of the year after reaching RMD age. See [[first-rmd-timing]].

### Risk level
The five-step rating on each fund's profile. See [[risk-levels]].

### RMD
Required minimum distribution. See [[rmd-overview]].

### Rollover
Moving retirement money from one plan or IRA to another. See [[rollovers-overview]].

### Round trip
A purchase followed by a sale of the same fund within 30 days.

@page glossary-s-z | Glossary S–Z
@summary Definitions of terms beginning S through Z.

## S

### SAI
Statement of Additional Information. See [[sai]].

### SEC
The Securities and Exchange Commission.

### SECURE Act / SECURE 2.0
Federal laws (2019, 2022) that changed RMD ages, inherited IRA rules, and more.

### SEP-IRA
Simplified Employee Pension IRA for self-employed people and small businesses. See [[sep-ira]].

### SEPP / 72(t)
Substantially equal periodic payments avoiding the early-withdrawal penalty. See [[sepp-72t]].

### Settlement
The completion of a trade, T+1 for mutual funds. See [[settlement-timing]].

### SIPC
Securities Investor Protection Corporation. See [[sipc-coverage]].

### Specific identification
A cost basis method in which the client chooses which shares to sell.

### Spousal IRA
An IRA funded based on a working spouse's income. See [[spousal-ira]].

### Standard deviation
A measure of how widely returns have varied.

### Step-up in basis
Resetting cost basis to the date-of-death value for inherited taxable assets. See [[inherited-taxable-accounts]].

### Suggested reply
The AI-drafted next message in the agent's support panel. See [[suggested-replies]].

### Systematic withdrawal plan (SWP)
Recurring withdrawals on a schedule. See [[systematic-withdrawals-overview]].

## T

### T+1
Settlement one business day after the trade date.

### Task expert
An AI specialist that collects the details for one service task. See [[task-experts]].

### TOD
Transfer on death registration for taxable accounts. See [[transfer-on-death]].

### Total return
Price change plus reinvested distributions. See [[total-return-vs-price]].

### Trusted contact person
Someone we may contact about exploitation or if we can't reach the client. See [[trusted-contact-person]].

### Turnover
The share of a fund's holdings replaced in a year.

## U–Z

### UTMA / UGMA
Uniform Transfers/Gifts to Minors Acts governing custodial accounts.

### Uniform Lifetime Table
The IRS table used for most owners' RMDs. See [[uniform-lifetime-table]].

### Value (style)
Companies that appear inexpensive relative to fundamentals.

### Wash sale
A loss disallowed because the same security was bought within 30 days. See [[wash-sales]].

### Wire
A same-day bank transfer. See [[incoming-wires]] and [[outgoing-wires]].

### Wrap-up code
The code classifying a chat's primary topic. See [[wrap-up-codes]].

### Yield
Income relative to price. See [[yields-explained]].

@page escalation-overview | When to escalate
@summary The situations that require escalation, to whom, and how fast.

## Escalation map

| Situation | Escalate to | Urgency |
|---|---|---|
| Suspected fraud or unauthorized activity | Fraud and Security, via team lead | Immediate |
| Suspected elder exploitation or diminished capacity | Senior Investor Protection | Immediate |
| Client asks for a supervisor | Team lead / supervisor | Immediate |
| Formal complaint, regulator or attorney mentioned | Supervisor and Compliance | Immediate |
| Death of an account holder | Estate Services | Same day |
| Investment advice requested | Licensed professional callback | At the client's chosen time |
| Complex IRA issue (excess contribution, recharacterization, 72(t)) | Retirement Specialists | Within 1 business day |
| Missing money (wire, ACH, check) | Money Movement Operations | Same day |
| Tax form error | Tax Reporting | Within 2 business days |
| Our processing error | Team lead, for correction approval | Same day |
| Legal documents (subpoena, court order, garnishment) | Legal | Same day; never handle in chat |
| Technical defect | Technical support log | As appropriate |

## How

Use the escalation tools in the workspace or notify your team lead. Always tell the client who will contact them, how, and when. Document the escalation in ACW.

## What escalation is not

Escalation is not a way to avoid a difficult conversation. Handle what you can; escalate what requires someone else's authority or expertise.

@page supervisor-escalation | Supervisor requests
@summary How to handle a client who asks for a supervisor.

## Steps

1. Don't resist or delay. "Of course. I'll get a supervisor for you."
2. Briefly ask what the client would like the supervisor to help with, so the supervisor is prepared. Don't make the client justify the request.
3. Notify your team lead with a one- or two-line summary.
4. Keep the client informed: "I've asked a supervisor to join. It may be a few minutes."
5. If no supervisor is available within 5 minutes, offer a supervisor callback within 1 business day.

## While waiting

Stay present. Don't argue the client's points. If the client asks you to continue helping in the meantime, do so.

## Example

Client: "I want to talk to your supervisor."

Agent: "Of course. I'll ask a supervisor to join. So they can help you right away, could you tell me in a sentence what you'd like them to look at?"

Client: "Your company lost my rollover check."

Agent: "Thank you. I've asked a supervisor to join and given them that summary. It may take a few minutes; I'll stay here with you."

## Documenting

Record the request, the reason, and the outcome in ACW. If a complaint was made, also follow [[complaints-handling]].

@page specialist-teams | Routing to specialist teams
@summary Which specialist team handles which request, and how to hand off cleanly.

## Teams and what they handle

| Team | Requests |
|---|---|
| Licensed professionals | Investment advice, portfolio reviews, planning conversations |
| Retirement Specialists | Excess contributions, recharacterizations, 72(t), complex rollovers, RMD calculation disputes, prior-year recoding |
| Estate Services | Deaths, inherited accounts, estate accounts, survivorship |
| Account Services | Registration changes, trust and custodial matters, POA review, name changes, address-unknown flags |
| Money Movement Operations | Wires, ACH issues, checks, stop payments, returned items |
| Tax Reporting | Corrected forms, cost basis issues |
| Fraud and Security | Unauthorized activity, compromised accounts |
| Senior Investor Protection | Exploitation, diminished capacity |
| Client Records | Older documents and transcripts |

See also [[department-directory]].

## A clean handoff

1. Summarize the client's request and what you've already confirmed.
2. Tell the client which team, how they'll be contacted, and when.
3. Make sure your ACW captures the referral (Referral wrap-up code where the referral is the main outcome).

## Choosing between a referral and a callback

- **Referral (internal)**: the specialist team works the request behind the scenes and contacts the client (excess contribution removals, stop payments, tax form corrections).
- **Callback**: the client wants a conversation (licensed advice, complex estate discussions).

## Avoiding ping-pong

Before referring, make sure the request truly belongs to that team. If you're unsure, ask your team lead. A client bounced between teams has a bad experience even if every team was helpful.

@page callback-scheduling | Scheduling callbacks
@summary How callbacks are scheduled through chat, availability, time zones, and what to confirm.

## How

Switch the column to the callback scope, or the client can schedule from the virtual assistant. The flow:

1. Opens with why a callback is the right next step (for example, licensed guidance).
2. Asks for the client's preferred day and time.
3. Checks availability and resolves the time correctly (callbacks are scheduled in Eastern Time; the server converts times safely, including across daylight saving changes).
4. Confirms the phone number to call.
5. Confirms the appointment.

## Availability

Callbacks are available during phone service hours: Monday–Friday 8:00 AM–8:00 PM ET and Saturday 9:00 AM–5:00 PM ET, excluding holidays. Licensed professional callbacks may have narrower availability. The flow offers only available slots.

## Time zones

Always state the time in the client's time zone and Eastern: "Thursday at 2:00 PM Eastern, which is 11:00 AM your time."

## What happens next

A dossier is prepared for the phone agent from the real conversation. See [[phone-callback-console]]. The client receives a confirmation.

## Changes

See [[cancel-reschedule-callback-task]].

## Example

Agent: "I'd suggest a call with one of our licensed professionals for this. What day and time works for you?"

Client: "Tomorrow after 3."

Agent: "I have 3:30 PM or 4:00 PM Eastern tomorrow. You're in Chicago, so that's 2:30 or 3:00 your time. Which do you prefer?"

## Phone number

Confirm the number to call. If the client wants a number not on file, note it for the callback only; changing their profile phone is a separate request.

@page cancel-reschedule-callback-task | Procedure: Cancel or Reschedule Callback (chat)
@summary Step-by-step chat procedure for changing an arranged callback.

## What the task collects

| Field | Options or format |
|---|---|
| Action | Cancel, Reschedule |
| New time | The new day and time (for Reschedule) |

## Procedure

1. Identify the callback: date and time currently scheduled.
2. Let the task expert collect the action and, for a reschedule, the new time.
3. Confirm the new time in both Eastern and the client's time zone, and the phone number.
4. Submit and confirm.

## Tips

- If the client wants to cancel because they no longer need help, ask if there's anything you can help with now.
- If the client wants an earlier time, check availability; same-day slots may be limited.

## Example

Client: "Can we move my call tomorrow to Friday morning?"

Agent: "Of course. Your call is set for tomorrow, Wednesday, at 2:00 PM Eastern. For Friday morning, would 10:00 AM Eastern work?"

After confirming: "Done. Your call is now Friday at 10:00 AM Eastern at the number ending in 0192."

## If the original time has passed

If the client missed a callback, schedule a new one rather than rescheduling. The phone agent will have noted the missed attempt.

@page warm-handoffs | Warm handoffs and transfers
@summary How to transfer a chat or hand off to a colleague so the client doesn't have to repeat themselves.

## Principles

- The client should never have to repeat their story.
- The receiving person should know what's been done and what's needed.

## How

1. Tell the client: "I'm going to connect you with our estate team, who handle this. I'll share what you've told me so you won't have to repeat it."
2. Send a short internal summary: client, request, facts confirmed, actions taken, what's needed.
3. Stay with the client until the handoff is complete, or until the callback is confirmed.

## Continued chats

If a client returns later, the "Continue this chat" feature loads prior context. See [[continued-chats]].

## What a good internal summary looks like

"Client: Maria Lopez. Wants to roll over a Fidelity 401(k) (~$85k, pre-tax and Roth portions) into Bob's. Has a Traditional IRA (acc-201) but no Roth IRA. Confirmed: she has requested paperwork from Fidelity. Needs: help opening a Roth IRA for the Roth portion and our receiving details. Prefers phone after 5 PM ET."

## What to tell the client

Tell the client what you've shared, who will contact them, and when. Clients who have had to repeat themselves are often frustrated; this sentence prevents that.

@page after-hours | After-hours requests
@summary What clients can do outside live-agent hours and how the virtual assistant handles requests.

## Available after hours

- The virtual assistant (24/7): answers, links, starting some tasks, scheduling callbacks.
- The portal and app: almost all self-service, including trades (priced at the next NAV).
- Secure messages (answered next business day).
- Phone service on Saturdays 9:00 AM–5:00 PM ET.

## Urgent after-hours issues

- Suspected fraud: clients can lock their online access from My Account → Security (or by repeated failed logins) and call when lines open. The virtual assistant provides fraud guidance.
- Lost or stolen device with the app: remove trusted devices online.

## Setting expectations

When a client chats near closing time, tell them honestly if their request can't be completed today and schedule a callback or note the follow-up.

## Example

A client chats at 7:50 PM ET wanting to change beneficiaries. You can complete it before closing if the details are simple. If not, tell them: "We close at 8:00 PM Eastern. I can schedule a callback for tomorrow morning, or you can update beneficiaries any time online at My Account → Beneficiaries."

## Trades after hours

Orders placed after 4:00 PM ET receive the next business day's NAV whether placed in chat, online, or on the weekend. Make this clear to clients chatting in the evening.

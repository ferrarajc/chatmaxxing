## Who we are

Bob's Mutual Funds is a mutual fund company and brokerage serving individual investors. We offer a lineup of 36 low-cost BobsFunds mutual funds and the accounts to hold them in: individual and joint taxable accounts, custodial accounts for minors, trust accounts, Traditional, Roth, SEP, and inherited IRAs. Clients invest online through the client portal, by phone, and through chat with our virtual assistant and service agents.

The firm was founded by Bob, an investor with sixty years in the markets whose views appear in the "Bob's View" opinion columns in the client Library. Our philosophy is straightforward: low costs, broad diversification, and patience are the most reliable drivers of long-term results.

## What we offer

- **36 mutual funds** across U.S. stocks, sector stocks, international stocks, and bonds, nearly all index funds with expense ratios from 0.03% to 0.13%. See [[fund-lineup-overview]].
- **No account fees**: no maintenance fee, no IRA custodial fee, and $0 to buy, sell, or exchange BobsFunds funds.
- **Self-service tools**: online account opening, automatic investing, dividend reinvestment, beneficiary management, tax documents, an RMD calculator, and a retirement calculator.
- **Education**: the Library of how-to guides and opinion columns, and a podcast.

## What we do not offer

We do not offer individual stocks, ETFs, options, other firms' mutual funds, bank accounts, credit cards, loans, insurance, 401(k) plan administration, 529 plans, or HSAs. We do not provide individualized investment advice through service channels. See [[products-not-offered]].

## Regulatory status

Bob's Mutual Funds is a registered broker-dealer and a member of FINRA and SIPC. The BobsFunds funds are registered investment companies. Our service is subject to FINRA rules, SEC regulations, and federal privacy and anti-money-laundering laws. For SIPC questions see [[sipc-coverage]].

## Answering "are you a real company / are you safe?"

Clients sometimes ask whether their money is safe with us. You can say: client assets are held in the client's name, separate from the firm's own assets; fund assets are held by an independent custodian; we are a FINRA and SIPC member. Do not say investments are "safe" or "guaranteed": fund values go up and down with markets.

## Market hours and trading

Mutual funds price once a day after the New York Stock Exchange closes at 4:00 PM ET. Orders can be entered online or through chat at any time, but they receive the NAV of the next close. An order placed Saturday morning receives Monday's NAV (or the next business day's). See [[trade-cutoff-nav]].

## After-hours requests

- Requests the virtual assistant cannot complete after hours can be turned into a scheduled callback.
- Secure messages sent after hours are answered on the next business day.
- Trades entered after 4:00 PM ET are processed at the next business day's NAV.

## Time zones

All our hours and cutoffs are Eastern Time. For a client in another time zone, convert explicitly: "Our 4:00 PM Eastern cutoff is 1:00 PM your time in California."

## Early closes

On **Friday, November 27** (the day after Thanksgiving) and **Thursday, December 24** (Christmas Eve), the stock market closes at 1:00 PM ET. The trade cutoff on those days is **1:00 PM ET**: orders received after 1:00 PM receive the next business day's NAV. Our service lines close at 3:00 PM ET.

## Bank holidays

Columbus Day (Monday, October 12) and Veterans Day (Wednesday, November 11) are bank holidays but not market holidays. Funds price and trades process normally, but ACH transfers and wires do not move on those days, so money movement is delayed by one business day.

## Talking to clients

Year-end deadlines (RMDs, Roth conversions, tax-loss sales) must be completed by the last business day of the year, which in 2026 is Thursday, December 31. Encourage clients to act well before the holiday week, since processing volume is heavy and mail is slow.

@page secure-messaging | Secure messages
@summary How clients send secure messages and documents, response times, and when to recommend a secure message over chat.

## What it is

Secure messaging lets authenticated clients send a message to Client Services from My Account → Messages. Messages are encrypted and stored in the client's message center. Clients can attach documents (PDF, JPG, or PNG, up to 10 MB each).

## Response times

- Standard requests: within **1 business day**.
- Complex account issues: within **3 business days**.
- Clients receive an email notification when a reply is waiting; the reply itself is only in the secure message center.

## When to recommend it

- The client needs to send a document (a trust certification, a death certificate copy, a divorce decree).
- The request needs research that will take longer than a chat.
- The client wants a written record of the answer.

## Limits

Secure messages are not for urgent matters. Fraud reports, locked-out clients, and time-sensitive trades should use chat or phone. We never ask for passwords by secure message.

## Example uses

- Sending a copy of a divorce decree for an IRA transfer.
- Asking for a cost basis history that requires research.
- Following up on an open request with a written trail.

## What agents see

Secure messages arrive in a separate service queue. If a client mentions in chat that they sent a secure message, you can check whether it has been answered and summarize the reply.

## Security

Clients should never email documents to staff. Secure messaging and the document upload page are the safe channels.

## Features

The mobile app (iOS and Android) supports viewing balances and holdings, buying, selling, and exchanging funds, managing automatic investments, depositing by ACH, viewing statements and tax documents, secure messages, chat, and sign-in approvals for 2FA. Account opening, beneficiary changes, and some forms are best done in the web portal.

## Common issues

| Issue | Suggestion |
|---|---|
| App won't open or crashes | Update to the latest version; restart the device |
| Biometric sign-in stopped working | Sign in with password once; re-enable Face ID / fingerprint in app settings |
| Not receiving sign-in approval prompts | Check notification permissions; ensure the device is still trusted |
| Balances look different from the website | Pull to refresh; values update after each day's NAV |

If an issue persists, collect the device type, app version, and a description, and log it as a Technical Issue. See [[chat-technical-issues]].

## Signing in

The app supports biometric sign-in (Face ID, fingerprint) after the first password sign-in. The device must be a trusted device. If the client gets a new phone, they sign in with their password and 2FA on the new device, then enable biometrics.

## Mobile check deposit

Checks up to $50,000 can be deposited in the app by photographing both sides. Endorse with the signature and "For mobile deposit to Bob's Mutual Funds only". Keep the paper check for 14 days, then destroy it. See [[checks-received]].

## Notifications

Push notifications can be enabled for trade confirmations, deposits, and security alerts in the app's settings.

@page sipc-coverage | SIPC and the safety of client assets
@summary What SIPC protects and does not protect, and how to answer questions about the safety of client assets.

## What SIPC is

The Securities Investor Protection Corporation (SIPC) protects customers of member broker-dealers if the firm fails financially and client securities or cash are missing. Coverage is up to $500,000 per customer per capacity, including up to $250,000 for cash.

## What SIPC does not cover

SIPC does not protect against market losses. If a fund's value declines, that is investment risk, not a loss covered by SIPC. SIPC also does not cover fraud by third parties outside the firm.

## How client assets are protected

- Client fund shares are registered in the client's name on the fund's records.
- Fund assets are held by an independent custodian bank, separate from Bob's Mutual Funds' corporate assets.
- Our broker-dealer is subject to SEC customer protection rules that require segregation of client assets.

## How to answer

"Your fund shares are held in your name, and the funds' assets are held by an independent custodian, separate from our company's money. We're also a member of SIPC, which protects customers if a brokerage firm fails, up to $500,000. SIPC doesn't protect against market ups and downs." Provide the SIPC website (sipc.org) if they want more detail.

Escalating complaints

Section: Compliance & Policies · Page ID: complaint-escalation

When and how to escalate a complaint to a supervisor or Compliance, and the response timelines.

On this page

  • Escalate immediately when
  • How
  • Timelines
  • Regulator references
  • Example
  • After escalation

Escalate immediately when

  • The client asks for a supervisor or manager.
  • The complaint alleges misconduct by an employee.
  • The complaint involves alleged fraud or unauthorized activity.
  • The client mentions an attorney, regulator (FINRA, SEC, state), or the media.
  • The client claims a financial loss and asks for compensation.
  • The client is a senior or vulnerable adult and the complaint involves a third party.

How

  1. Tell the client you are escalating and what to expect.
  2. Notify your team lead in the workspace (or by the escalation channel).
  3. If the supervisor joins, brief them in a sentence or two.
  4. If no supervisor is immediately available, schedule a supervisor callback within 1 business day.

Timelines

  • Acknowledgment: same day.
  • Supervisor contact: within 1 business day.
  • Resolution or written response: typically within 5 business days for routine complaints; Compliance handles complex cases.

Regulator references

If a client says they will complain to FINRA or the SEC, do not discourage them. They have the right to. Log it and escalate.

Example

Client: "If this isn't fixed, I'm reporting you to FINRA."

Agent: "I understand, and you have every right to contact FINRA. I want to help resolve this too. I've logged your complaint and asked a supervisor to contact you by tomorrow."

After escalation

Note the complaint and the escalation in ACW, with the complaint reference.

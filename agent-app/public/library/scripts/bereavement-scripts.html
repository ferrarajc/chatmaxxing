Bereavement

Section: Scripts & Templates · Page ID: bereavement-scripts

Compassionate language for conversations about a death.

On this page

  • Templates
  • When the caller is also grieving the client's spouse
  • When the client needs money urgently
  • When the caller is upset by a requirement

Templates

Opening: "I'm so sorry for the loss of your father. I'm here to help, and we'll take this one step at a time."

Explaining next steps: "Here's what happens next: we'll protect your father's accounts while we review documents, and our Estate Services team will contact you within two business days. They'll send a checklist of what's needed, which usually starts with a certified copy of the death certificate. You don't need to decide anything today."

Information you can't share yet: "I understand you'd like to know what's in the account. Once Estate Services has the documents confirming your role, they'll be able to go over everything with you."

Closing: "Please take care of yourself. If anything comes up before Estate Services reaches you, you can message us any time."

See Communicating with grieving clients.

When the caller is also grieving the client's spouse

"I'm so sorry about Robert. I know this is a lot to handle. Let's take care of what's most urgent for you today, and we'll connect you with our Estate Services team for the rest."

When the client needs money urgently

"I understand you need funds for expenses right now. Let's look at your own accounts first. For Robert's accounts, Estate Services can explain what can be released and how quickly."

When the caller is upset by a requirement

"I know asking for a death certificate feels like one more burden right now. It's how we make sure his assets go exactly where he intended, and it protects you and your family."

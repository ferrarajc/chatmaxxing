Verification

Section: Scripts & Templates · Page ID: verification-scripts

Language for additional identity verification that feels protective, not suspicious.

On this page

  • Templates
  • Avoid
  • Example: a request to change email and withdraw
  • Example: the owner is present and asks you to speak with a family member

Templates

Before a high-risk request: "Before I make this change, I need to verify a couple of details to protect your account. Could you tell me the ZIP code on file and the last four digits of your Social Security number?"

Third party: "I'm glad to help with general questions. For account-specific information, I can only speak with the account owner or someone they've authorized. If your mother would like you to have access, she can add you as an authorized user."

Verification failed: "For your security, I'm not able to make changes in chat right now. Please call us at 1-800-BOB-FUND so we can verify your identity by phone."

Avoid

Saying which detail was wrong. Reading out personal information for the client to confirm.

Example: a request to change email and withdraw

"I can help with both. Because you're updating your email and taking money out in the same conversation, I need to verify a couple of extra details first. This is to protect your account. Could you tell me your date of birth and the ZIP code we have on file?"

Example: the owner is present and asks you to speak with a family member

"I'm happy to talk with your son about your account while you're here in the chat. Just so I have it on record, you'd like me to discuss your account with him today, is that right?"

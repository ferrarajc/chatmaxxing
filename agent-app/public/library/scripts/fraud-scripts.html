Fraud and security concerns

Section: Scripts & Templates · Page ID: fraud-scripts

Language for clients reporting suspicious activity or scams.

On this page

  • Templates
  • Client already sent money to a scammer
  • Client can't tell whether a call was from us

Templates

Client reports unauthorized activity: "Thank you for letting us know right away. I'm going to help you secure your account now and get our security team involved."

Phishing: "That email didn't come from us. We'll never ask for your password or a security code. Please don't click any links in it. If you entered your password anywhere, let's reset it now."

Pausing a suspicious request: "This request needs an additional security review before it can be completed. Our security team will contact you at the phone number on file, usually within one business day."

Wire scam warning: "Our wire instructions never change by email. If you received a message saying they changed, please don't send money to that account."

See Suspected account takeover or unauthorized activity and Phishing and investment scams.

Client already sent money to a scammer

"I'm sorry this happened. Let's act quickly. I'm escalating to our security team now to see what can be done, and I'd recommend you also contact your bank right away and report the scam to the authorities."

Client can't tell whether a call was from us

"If you're ever unsure whether a call is really from us, hang up and call 1-800-BOB-FUND directly. We'll never be offended."

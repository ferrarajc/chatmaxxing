Greetings

Section: Scripts & Templates · Page ID: greeting-scripts

Opening messages for new, escalated, and continued chats.

On this page

  • Principles
  • Templates
  • Avoid
  • Matching the client's energy
  • Using pronouns and names

Principles

Greet by name, show you know why they're here, and move to the task. The suggested greeting is drafted from the intent; edit as needed.

Templates

Escalated from the virtual assistant (intent known): "Hi Maria, I'm Jordan. I see you'd like to roll over a 401(k) from a previous employer. I can help with that. Do you already have an IRA with us you'd like it to go into?"

Intent unclear: "Hi Sam, I'm Jordan from Bob's Mutual Funds. How can I help you today?"

Continued chat: "Welcome back, David. Last time we set up your monthly investment into BF500. How can I help today?"

After a wait: "Hi Priya, thanks for your patience. I'm Jordan, and I'm here to help with your RMD question."

Avoid

"How may I assist you?" when the intent is already known. Long introductions. Asking the client to repeat what they told the virtual assistant.

Matching the client's energy

If the client's first message to the virtual assistant was terse or frustrated, keep your greeting short and get to the point. If they were chatty, a little warmth goes a long way.

Using pronouns and names

Use the name the client uses for themselves in the chat. If their profile lists pronouns, use them when referring to the client in notes or handoffs.

Declining advice requests

Section: Scripts & Templates · Page ID: advice-decline-scripts

Language that declines investment advice warmly and offers real help instead.

Templates

General: "That's an important decision, and it depends on your whole financial picture, so I'm not able to recommend a specific investment. I can explain how the options work, or set up a call with one of our licensed professionals who can give you personalized guidance. Which would you prefer?"

"What would you do?": "I appreciate you asking! I'm not able to give personal recommendations, but I can walk you through the facts on each fund side by side."

Choosing between funds: "Here's how BF500 and BFTM compare on cost, holdings, and history. Which fits your goals is something a licensed professional can help you decide; I'm happy to set up that call."

Timing the market: "No one can reliably predict where markets will go next. I can share how the fund has behaved historically, and if you'd like guidance on your strategy, I can schedule a call with a licensed professional."

Roth vs. Traditional: "Both have advantages, and the right choice depends on your tax situation now versus in retirement. I can explain how each works, or arrange a call with a licensed professional."

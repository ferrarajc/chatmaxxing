Scripts & Templates

Ready-to-adapt language for greetings, verification, holds, bereavement, complaints, declining advice, closings, market volatility, fraud, technical issues, confirmation recaps, and handoffs.

12 pages in this section.

  1. Greetings — Opening messages for new, escalated, and continued chats.
  2. Verification — Language for additional identity verification that feels protective, not suspicious.
  3. Holds and delays — Messages for when you need time to research or a process takes longer than expected.
  4. Bereavement — Compassionate language for conversations about a death.
  5. Complaints and de-escalation — Language for acknowledging frustration, gathering facts, logging complaints, and escalating.
  6. Declining advice requests — Language that declines investment advice warmly and offers real help instead.
  7. Closing a chat — Messages for ending chats, including summaries of what was done and next steps.
  8. Market volatility — Language for anxious clients during market declines.
  9. Fraud and security concerns — Language for clients reporting suspicious activity or scams.
  10. Technical issues — Language for helping clients with website, app, or chat problems.
  11. Confirmation recaps — How to recap a task before submitting so the client's confirmation is clear and complete.
  12. Handoffs and referrals — Language for routing a client to another team or a callback.

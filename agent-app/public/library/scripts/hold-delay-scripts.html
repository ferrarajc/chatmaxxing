Holds and delays

Section: Scripts & Templates · Page ID: hold-delay-scripts

Messages for when you need time to research or a process takes longer than expected.

On this page

  • Templates
  • Avoid
  • Long waits
  • Explaining delays outside our control

Templates

Researching: "Good question. I want to give you an exact answer, so let me check. This will take about two minutes."

Still checking: "Thanks for bearing with me. I'm still confirming this and should have an answer in a minute or two."

Need to follow up later: "I want to get this exactly right, so I'm going to confirm with our retirement specialists. You'll hear back from me by secure message by 3:00 PM Eastern tomorrow."

Process delay (for example, a transfer): "I can see the transfer request is still with your previous firm. These usually complete within 5 to 7 business days. If it hasn't arrived by Friday, let us know and we'll follow up with them."

Avoid

Disappearing without a message. Vague promises ("soon"). Blaming other teams.

Long waits

If research will take more than 5 minutes, give the client a choice: "This will take a bit longer to confirm. Would you like to wait, or should I send you the answer by secure message within the hour?"

Explaining delays outside our control

"Your previous employer's plan has to release the money first. Once they send it, it typically arrives within 5 to 15 business days. I'll note your account so we watch for it."

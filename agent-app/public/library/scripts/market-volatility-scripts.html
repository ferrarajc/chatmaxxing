Market volatility

Section: Scripts & Templates · Page ID: market-volatility-scripts

Language for anxious clients during market declines.

On this page

  • Templates
  • Avoid
  • Client asks "Should I sell?"
  • Client is clearly distressed

Templates

Acknowledge: "It's completely understandable to feel uneasy when you see your balance drop."

Explain factually: "Stock funds broadly declined this week, and BF500 follows the 500 largest U.S. companies, so it moved with them."

Context: "Markets have gone through many declines; for example, BF500 fell 18.2% in 2022 and then rose 26.3% in 2023. Of course, past performance doesn't guarantee future results."

Offer options: "If you'd like to talk through your strategy, I can schedule a call with a licensed professional. If you've decided on a change, I can help you make it."

Avoid

"It'll come back." "Don't sell." "Now's a buying opportunity." Any prediction.

See Talking with clients during market downturns.

Client asks "Should I sell?"

"That's a personal decision, and I can't recommend whether to sell. What I can do is explain how a sale would work, including any tax effects, or set up a call with a licensed professional who can talk through your plan. Which would help most?"

Client is clearly distressed

Slow down. Lead with empathy, offer the callback, and do not rush any transaction. If the client still wants to sell, confirm clearly and process it.

Confirmation recaps

Section: Scripts & Templates · Page ID: confirmation-recap-scripts

How to recap a task before submitting so the client's confirmation is clear and complete.

On this page

  • The rule
  • Templates
  • Avoid

The rule

Before any action is submitted, recap every material detail in one message and get a clear yes. The evidence highlighting uses this confirmed recap.

Templates

Purchase: "To confirm: buy $2,000 of BobsFunds 500 Index (BF500) in your Roth IRA, funded from your linked bank account ending in 4421, as a 2026 contribution. Shall I place it?"

Sale: "To confirm: sell $5,000 of BobsFunds Growth (BFGR) in your taxable account, with the proceeds kept as cash in the account. Is that right?"

Exchange: "To confirm: exchange $3,000 from BFGR into BobsFunds Bond Income (BFBI) in your Traditional IRA. Shall I go ahead?"

Beneficiary: "To confirm, after this change your Roth IRA's primary beneficiaries will be David Chen (spouse) at 60% and Maria Chen (daughter) at 40%, with no secondary beneficiaries. Is that correct?"

Distribution: "To confirm: withdraw $5,000 from your Traditional IRA, withhold 10% federal tax, and deposit $4,500 to your linked bank. Shall I submit it?"

Avoid

Recapping only part of the details. Treating "ok" to a different question as confirmation. Submitting after the client changes a detail without a fresh recap.

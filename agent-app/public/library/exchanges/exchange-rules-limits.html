Exchange rules and limits

Section: Exchanges · Page ID: exchange-rules-limits

What can be exchanged, between which accounts, and the situations where an exchange isn't possible.

On this page

  • Allowed
  • Not allowed or not an exchange
  • Restrictions

Allowed

  • Any BobsFunds fund to any other BobsFunds fund in the same account.
  • Partial or full exchanges.
  • Several exchanges on the same day (each is a separate transaction).

Not allowed or not an exchange

RequestWhy notWhat to do instead
Move money from a taxable account to an IRADifferent accounts and tax typesSell in the taxable account and make an IRA contribution (subject to limits)
Move money from an IRA to a taxable accountThat is a distributionRequest a distribution
Move from a Traditional IRA to a Roth IRAThat is a conversionSee Roth conversions: overview
Move between two of the client's taxable accountsDifferent accountsA journal transfer between accounts (Account Services), or sell and buy
Exchange into another company's fundWe hold only BobsFundsNot available
Exchange while purchase payment is pendingAllowed within the accountThe redemption hold still applies to withdrawals

Restrictions

A client restricted under the frequent-trading policy cannot exchange into the restricted fund for 30 days. A foreign address can block exchanges into funds as new purchases. See Non-U.S. persons and clients living abroad.

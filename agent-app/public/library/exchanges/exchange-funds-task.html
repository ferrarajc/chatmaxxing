Procedure: Exchange Between Funds (chat)

Section: Exchanges · Page ID: exchange-funds-task

Step-by-step chat procedure for an exchange, the fields collected, and the checks before approving.

On this page

  • What the task collects
  • Procedure
  • Requests that aren't exchanges

What the task collects

FieldOptions or format
AccountOne of the client's accounts (asked if more than one)
Fund to exchange out ofA fund held in that account
Fund to exchange intoAny BobsFunds fund
Amount to exchangeA dollar amount, or the full balance of the "from" fund

Procedure

  1. Confirm the request in one sentence: "You'd like to move $3,000 from BFGR into BFBI in your Traditional IRA. Is that right?"
  2. Let the task expert collect the details, or choose Exchange Between Funds from the task menu.
  3. Check the Proposed Action:

- "From" fund is held in the account and the amount is no more than its value. - "To" fund is the one the client described. Watch out for similar names (BFST Short-Term Treasury versus BFSTC Short-Term Corporate versus BFSTP Consumer Staples). - "From" and "to" are not the same fund. - In a taxable account, the client knows an exchange is a taxable sale. See Tax effects of exchanges. - The exchange does not violate the frequent-trading policy. See Frequent trading (market timing) policy.

  1. Submit. The client receives a green confirmation card.
  2. Tell the client the exchange will be priced at today's NAVs (if before 4:00 PM ET).

Requests that aren't exchanges

  • Moving between accounts: see Exchange rules and limits.
  • "Rebalance my account": see Rebalancing requests.
  • "Move everything to safety": an emotional request in a falling market. Process it if it is clearly what the client wants, without commentary; if they ask your opinion, see Talking with clients during market downturns.

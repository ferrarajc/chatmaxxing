Choosing a channel for the client

  • If the client is locked out of online access, chat cannot help them authenticate. Give them the phone number.
  • If the client needs to send documents (death certificate, trust certification), they can upload through a secure message or the document upload page, or mail certified copies.
  • If the client needs licensed advice, schedule a callback.
  • If the client prefers to talk, offer a callback rather than asking them to call and wait.

Language and accessibility

Phone service is available in Spanish, and interpretation for other languages can be arranged. Clients who are deaf or hard of hearing can use their preferred relay service to call our main number. See Language services and Accessibility and accommodations.

Holiday calendar 2026

Section: Company & Contact Directory · Page ID: holiday-calendar-2026

Market and service holidays

On these days the New York Stock Exchange is closed, BobsFunds funds do not price, and our service lines (chat agents and phone) are closed. The virtual assistant and online portal remain available.

Early closes

On Friday, November 27 (the day after Thanksgiving) and Thursday, December 24 (Christmas Eve), the stock market closes at 1:00 PM ET. The trade cutoff on those days is 1:00 PM ET: orders received after 1:00 PM receive the next business day's NAV. Our service lines close at 3:00 PM ET.

Bank holidays

Columbus Day (Monday, October 12) and Veterans Day (Wednesday, November 11) are bank holidays but not market holidays. Funds price and trades process normally, but ACH transfers and wires do not move on those days, so money movement is delayed by one business day.

Talking to clients

Year-end deadlines (RMDs, Roth conversions, tax-loss sales) must be completed by the last business day of the year, which in 2026 is Thursday, December 31. Encourage clients to act well before the holiday week, since processing volume is heavy and mail is slow.

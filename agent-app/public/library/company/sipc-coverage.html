SIPC and the safety of client assets

Section: Company & Contact Directory · Page ID: sipc-coverage

What SIPC protects and does not protect, and how to answer questions about the safety of client assets.

On this page

  • What SIPC is
  • What SIPC does not cover
  • How client assets are protected
  • How to answer

What SIPC is

The Securities Investor Protection Corporation (SIPC) protects customers of member broker-dealers if the firm fails financially and client securities or cash are missing. Coverage is up to $500,000 per customer per capacity, including up to $250,000 for cash.

What SIPC does not cover

SIPC does not protect against market losses. If a fund's value declines, that is investment risk, not a loss covered by SIPC. SIPC also does not cover fraud by third parties outside the firm.

How client assets are protected

  • Client fund shares are registered in the client's name on the fund's records.
  • Fund assets are held by an independent custodian bank, separate from Bob's Mutual Funds' corporate assets.
  • Our broker-dealer is subject to SEC customer protection rules that require segregation of client assets.

How to answer

"Your fund shares are held in your name, and the funds' assets are held by an independent custodian, separate from our company's money. We're also a member of SIPC, which protects customers if a brokerage firm fails, up to $500,000. SIPC doesn't protect against market ups and downs." Provide the SIPC website (sipc.org) if they want more detail.

Paper statement fee

Section: Statements & Fees · Page ID: paper-fees

The $2 monthly paper statement fee, who pays it, the waivers, and how to help clients avoid it.

The fee

$2 per month per account for clients who receive statements by mail. It is charged quarterly ($6) from the account's cash or, if there is no cash, by selling a small amount of the largest holding.

Waivers

  • Paperless delivery of statements.
  • Clients aged 75 or older.
  • Accounts with a balance over $250,000.
  • Accounts with an accessibility accommodation for printed documents.

Avoiding it

Switching to paperless at My Account → Preferences stops the fee from the next quarter. See Paperless delivery.

Refunds

If a client qualified for a waiver that was not applied (for example, they turned 75), refund the fees through a fee adjustment request (team lead approval). See Fee disputes and adjustments.

Example

A client with two accounts, both receiving paper statements, pays $2 × 2 accounts × 3 months = $12 per quarter. Switching to paperless stops it from the next quarter.

How the fee appears

It appears in the account activity as "Paper statement fee" each quarter, and on the statement.

Clients who prefer paper

Some clients prefer paper and are willing to pay; that is their choice. Don't pressure them. Mention the waivers in case they qualify (age 75+, balances over $250,000).

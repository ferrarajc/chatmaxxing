Inherited IRA

Section: Account Types · Page ID: inherited-ira

The account that holds IRA assets for a beneficiary after the owner dies, and how its distribution rules depend on the beneficiary type.

On this page

  • What it is
  • Distribution rules depend on the beneficiary
  • Taxes and penalties
  • Setting one up

What it is

When an IRA owner dies, beneficiaries usually move their share into an inherited IRA (sometimes called a beneficiary IRA) titled, for example, "Jane Smith, deceased (date), FBO John Smith, beneficiary". The assets stay tax-deferred (or tax-free for an inherited Roth) while the beneficiary takes distributions under special rules. No new contributions are allowed.

Distribution rules depend on the beneficiary

BeneficiaryMain options
Surviving spouseTreat as their own IRA, roll over to their own IRA, or keep as an inherited IRA
Eligible designated beneficiary (minor child of the owner, disabled, chronically ill, or not more than 10 years younger than the owner)Distributions over life expectancy (a minor child switches to the 10-year rule at 21)
Other individual (designated beneficiary)Must empty the account by the end of the 10th year after death; annual RMDs in years 1–9 if the owner had started RMDs
Estate, charity, or non-qualifying trust5-year rule if the owner died before RMDs began; otherwise over the owner's remaining life expectancy

Details: Options for a surviving spouse, Options for non-spouse beneficiaries, Eligible designated beneficiaries, Inherited IRA RMDs.

Taxes and penalties

Distributions from an inherited Traditional IRA are taxable income but are never subject to the 10% early-withdrawal penalty, whatever the beneficiary's age. Inherited Roth distributions are generally tax-free if the original owner's Roth had met the five-year requirement.

Setting one up

Inherited IRAs are set up by Estate Services after the death is reported and documentation is received. Chat agents should not open inherited IRAs through the regular account-opening task. See When someone reports a death.

SEP-IRA

Section: Account Types · Page ID: sep-ira

The Simplified Employee Pension IRA for self-employed people and small businesses: who can open one, contribution rules, and deadlines.

On this page

  • Who it is for
  • Contributions
  • Other rules
  • Opening a SEP-IRA

Who it is for

A SEP-IRA lets self-employed individuals and small-business owners make tax-deductible retirement contributions for themselves and their eligible employees. Freelancers, consultants, sole proprietors, partners, and owners of small corporations use them.

Contributions

  • Contributions are made by the employer (for a sole proprietor, that is the client acting as their own employer).
  • The maximum is 25% of compensation (for a self-employed person, effectively about 20% of net self-employment earnings after the deduction for half of self-employment tax), up to $72,000 for 2026 ($70,000 for 2025). Compensation above $360,000 (2026) is disregarded.
  • If the business has eligible employees, it must contribute the same percentage of pay for each eligible employee as for the owner.
  • Contributions are discretionary: the business can contribute nothing in a lean year.
  • The deadline is the business's tax-filing deadline, including extensions. See SEP-IRA contributions.

Other rules

  • Withdrawals, taxes, the 10% early-withdrawal penalty, and RMDs work the same as a Traditional IRA.
  • SEP-IRA balances can be converted to Roth.
  • A SEP-IRA owner may also have a Traditional or Roth IRA and make regular IRA contributions to it, subject to the regular limits.

Opening a SEP-IRA

The client opens a SEP-IRA for themselves online or through chat. Businesses with employees must also adopt a SEP plan document (IRS Form 5305-SEP); employees each open their own SEP-IRA. See Opening IRAs.

Common questions

How much can I put in my SEP?

The calculation depends on the client's net self-employment income and is a tax question. Explain the 25%-of-compensation rule and the dollar cap, and suggest they confirm the exact figure with their tax preparer.

Should I use a SEP or a Solo 401(k)?

We do not offer Solo 401(k) plans. The client Library has a factual comparison article (SEP-IRA vs. Solo 401(k)); you may send it, but the choice is a planning decision.

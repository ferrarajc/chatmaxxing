"I can't see one of my accounts"

Section: Troubleshooting · Page ID: cant-see-account

Why an account might not appear in a client's portal view, and how to fix it.

On this page

  • Common reasons
  • Fixing it
  • Before you change anything
  • Joint owners
  • Custodial and trust accounts
  • Closed accounts
  • Duplicate profiles

Common reasons

  • The account is under a different login: a joint account opened by the other owner, a custodial account under the custodian, a trust account under the trustee login, or a second profile created with a different email.
  • The account was closed (zero balance for 12 months closes automatically).
  • New account still pending: application not yet approved.
  • Estate or protective hold restricting access.
  • Authorized-user access removed by the owner.

Fixing it

Linking profiles (for example, a client with two logins) is done by Account Services after verification. Joint owners each have their own login; they can link the joint account to their profile through My Account → Add Existing Account.

Before you change anything

Verify the client's identity carefully. A request to "add an account to my login" is a request to give someone access to assets, so it must come from an owner of that account.

Joint owners

Each joint owner has their own login. If the second owner can't see a joint account, they can add it from My Account → Add Existing Account by entering the account number and verifying with their own SSN; the system confirms they are an owner.

Custodial and trust accounts

The custodian or trustee sees these accounts under their own login. The minor or trust beneficiary does not.

Closed accounts

Closed accounts disappear from the portfolio view, but their documents remain at My Account → Statements & Documents for 7 years.

Duplicate profiles

Clients who opened accounts years apart with different email addresses may have two profiles. Account Services can merge them after verification, so all accounts appear under one login.

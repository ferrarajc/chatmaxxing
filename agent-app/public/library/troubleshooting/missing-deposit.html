"My deposit isn't showing"

Section: Troubleshooting · Page ID: missing-deposit

A diagnostic path for deposits that a client expected but doesn't see, by method.

On this page

  • Ask
  • By method
  • Next steps

Ask

  • How was the money sent (ACH from a linked bank, wire, check, transfer from another firm, rollover)?
  • When, and how much?
  • Into which account?

By method

MethodWhat to checkTypical cause
ACH purchaseRecent transactions for the purchase; the client's bank shows the debit 2–3 business days laterThe shares are credited before the bank is debited, so a client watching their bank may be confused; or the order was placed after 4:00 PM
Automatic investmentPlan status, the scheduled day, bank linkPaused plan, weekend/holiday, returned payment
WireWire received but unmatchedMissing account number or name mismatch; ask for the federal reference number. See Incoming wires
CheckMail time; whether the check was acceptableStill in the mail; a third-party or starter check returned
Mobile check depositDeposit status in the appUnder review; image quality
ACAT transferTransfer statusRejected for mismatch; still in progress. See Tracking rollovers and transfers
RolloverWhether the plan sent it, how, whenPlan still processing; check mailed to the client

Next steps

If the money left the client's bank or the other institution more than 2 business days ago and nothing appears, escalate to Money Movement Operations with the details (amount, date, method, reference or trace numbers). Tell the client what you've done and when they'll hear back.

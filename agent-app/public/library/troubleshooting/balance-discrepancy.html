"My balance looks wrong"

Section: Troubleshooting · Page ID: balance-discrepancy

Common reasons a balance differs from what the client expects and how to explain each.

On this page

  • Common reasons
  • How to explain
  • If something is truly wrong
  • Example

Common reasons

ReasonExplanation
Market movementValues change daily with NAVs
A distribution dateThe NAV dropped by the distribution; the value moved to cash or new shares. See Record, ex-dividend, reinvestment, and pay dates
Pending transactionsA purchase or sale placed today isn't priced until tonight
Cash versus investedThe total includes uninvested cash; the client may be looking at holdings only
Statement dateStatements show period-end values
WithholdingAn IRA distribution was reduced by tax withholding
A feePaper statement fee, wire fee
A returned paymentA purchase was reversed
Looking at the wrong accountClients with several accounts

How to explain

Walk through the activity since the date the client remembers: "On the 1st your account was $41,200. Since then, BF500 paid a dividend that was reinvested, and the market fell about 2%, which brings it to today's $40,600."

If something is truly wrong

If after review the numbers don't reconcile (a missing transaction, a duplicate), escalate with specifics. See Duplicate or unexpected transactions.

Example

Client: "My statement said $41,986 but now the website says $41,650. Where did $336 go?"

Agent: "Your statement shows September 30. Since then, the market has moved: BF500, your largest holding, is down a little over 1% this month, which accounts for about $330. There have been no withdrawals or fees."

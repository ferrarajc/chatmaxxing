"I'm not getting your emails"

Section: Troubleshooting · Page ID: email-not-received

Why clients don't receive our emails and how to fix it.

On this page

  • Checks
  • Password reset emails
  • Statement and tax form notifications
  • Security codes by email
  • Changing the email address
  • Phishing caution

Checks

  • Is the email address on file correct and verified? (Ask the client to tell you the address.)
  • Spam or junk folders; add noreply@bobsmutualfunds.com to safe senders.
  • Corporate or school email filters.
  • Did the client unsubscribe from that type of email? Security alerts and document notifications can't be unsubscribed, but marketing can.
  • Bounced emails: if our emails bounce, paperless delivery is switched to paper and the client is notified by mail.

Password reset emails

Reset links expire after 30 minutes and only the latest link works. See Password resets.

Statement and tax form notifications

Paperless clients receive a notification email when documents are ready. If they missed one, the document is still available online at My Account → Statements & Documents. Offer to send the link.

Security codes by email

Some verification codes are sent by email. Codes expire after 10 minutes; ask the client to request a new one and to check that they are looking at the newest message.

Changing the email address

If the client's email address has changed, they can update it at My Account → Profile and confirm the code sent to the new address. See Email and phone changes.

Phishing caution

Clients sometimes find an email that looks like ours in their spam folder and are unsure whether it is genuine. Our emails never ask for passwords and never contain account numbers. If in doubt, they should sign in directly rather than clicking a link. See Phishing and investment scams.

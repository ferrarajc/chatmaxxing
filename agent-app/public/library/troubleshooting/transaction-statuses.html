Transaction statuses

Section: Troubleshooting · Page ID: transaction-statuses

The five transaction statuses clients see, what each means, and how long each lasts.

The lifecycle

Mutual funds price once a day and settle the next business day, so every order moves through the same stages:

StatusMeaningTypical duration
ScheduledA future-dated transaction (an automatic investment or withdrawal, or an order placed on a weekend or holiday)Until its date
PendingPlaced and waiting for tonight's NAVUntil about 6:00 PM ET on the trade date
SettlingExecuted at the NAV, in T+1 settlementOne business day
CompletedSettled and posted to the accountFinal
CanceledCancelled by the client before the cutoff, or reversed (for example, a returned payment)Final

Common questions

"My purchase says pending. Did it go through?"

Yes, it was received. It will be priced at today's closing NAV (if placed before 4:00 PM ET) and show the number of shares tonight.

"Why is it 'settling' if I already have the shares?"

The shares are credited when the trade is priced; settlement is the back-office completion the next business day.

"My order was canceled. I didn't cancel it."

Check the reason: a returned payment, an IRA contribution over the limit, a frequent-trading block, or an incomplete order. See Returned and failed purchases.

Reference numbers

Orders placed in chat have a reference number (REF-XXXXXX) shown on the confirmation card. See Reference numbers.

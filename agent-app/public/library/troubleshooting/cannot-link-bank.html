"I can't link my bank"

Section: Troubleshooting · Page ID: cannot-link-bank

Troubleshooting failed bank linking by instant verification and micro-deposits.

On this page

  • Micro-deposits issues
  • Name or ownership mismatch

  • After linking

Instant verification fails

  • The bank may not support it or may require additional security steps. Use micro-deposits instead.
  • The client's online banking credentials or multi-factor prompt failed; try again on the bank's own site first.

Micro-deposits issues

IssueFix
Deposits haven't appearedAllow 2 business days; check the correct bank account; savings accounts may show them differently
Wrong amounts entered three timesVerification locked; remove and start again
More than 14 days passedStart again

Name or ownership mismatch

The bank account must belong to an owner of the Bob's account. Business accounts and relatives' accounts cannot be linked. See Linking bank accounts.

Bank account types

Checking and savings accounts at U.S. banks and credit unions are supported. Prepaid cards, some online-only "neobank" accounts, and brokerage cash accounts may not support ACH debits.

Business and joint bank accounts

  • A business bank account can be linked only to a business-related registration (for example, a sole proprietor's SEP-IRA contributions from their business account are allowed if the client is the account holder of record).
  • A joint bank account can be linked if at least one owner of the bank account is an owner of the Bob's account.

After linking

Remind the client about the 10-day rule: a newly linked bank can fund purchases right away, but electronic withdrawals to it need a verification call in the first 10 days. See Linking bank accounts.

Alternatives while linking is pending

If the client needs to invest today, a wire is the fastest alternative; for slower needs, a check by mail works. See Funding a new account.

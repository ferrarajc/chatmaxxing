Closing an account

Section: Account Maintenance · Page ID: closing-an-account

How a client closes an account, what happens to the money, taxes and penalties, and retention conversations.

How to close

  1. The client sells all holdings in the account (a full redemption) or transfers them to another account or firm.
  2. The proceeds are paid out by ACH, check, or wire, or moved to another Bob's account.
  3. Once the balance is zero and no transactions are pending, the client requests closure at My Account → Account Settings → Close Account, or Account Services closes it on request.

Accounts with a zero balance for 12 months are closed automatically.

IRAs

Closing an IRA means taking a full distribution (taxable, possibly penalized) or transferring it to another IRA custodian (not taxable). Make sure the client knows the difference. A client moving their IRA to another firm should start the transfer from the new firm rather than taking a distribution. See Outgoing transfers to another firm and IRA distributions overview.

Things to check

  • Pending automatic investments or withdrawals: they should be cancelled.
  • Dividends declared but not yet paid: a small residual may arrive after closing; we mail a check for it.
  • Taxes: a sale in a taxable account may produce a gain or loss; the client will receive a 1099-B. IRA distributions produce a 1099-R.
  • Tax forms for the year will still be produced and delivered.

Retention conversations

It is appropriate to ask, once and without pressure, why the client is leaving, and to address any service issue. Do not argue, delay, or make closing difficult. If the client is leaving because of a complaint, handle it as a complaint. See Handling complaints.

Fees

We do not charge an account closing fee or an outgoing transfer fee. An outgoing wire for the proceeds costs $25.

Procedure: Update Contact Information

Section: Account Maintenance · Page ID: contact-info-updates

Chat procedure for changing a client's phone number, email address, or mailing address, with the fraud checks that go with it.

What the task collects

FieldOptions or format
What to updatePhone number, Email address, Mailing address
New valueThe new phone number, email address, or full mailing address

Why contact changes are sensitive

A change of email, phone, or address is the first step in most account takeovers: a fraudster changes the contact details so that security alerts and password reset links go to them, then moves money. That is why we send a notice to both the old and the new contact point, and why some money movement is restricted for a short period after a change.

Procedure

  1. Confirm the chat is authenticated and nothing suggests a third party. If the client is also asking to move money or add a bank in the same conversation, re-verify identity first. See Verifying client identity.
  2. Let the task expert collect what to update and the new value, or start the task from the task menu.
  3. Read the new value back exactly as collected:

- Phone: all ten digits, and whether it is mobile (needed for text-message 2FA). - Email: spell out unusual parts. A typo means the client stops receiving statements and reset links. - Address: street, unit, city, state, ZIP. A PO Box can be a mailing address but not a residential address.

  1. Get a clear confirmation, review the Proposed Action, and submit.
  2. Tell the client what happens next (below).

What happens next

  • Email: a verification code is sent to the new address. The client confirms it at My Account → Profile; until verified, statements and alerts continue to the old address. A notice of the change goes to the old address.
  • Phone: the new number must be verified by a code before it can be used for 2FA text messages.
  • Address: takes effect immediately for mail. A notice goes to both the old and new addresses.

Restrictions after a change

  • For 15 days after an address change, redemption checks cannot be mailed to the new address without a medallion signature guarantee. See Medallion signature guarantees.
  • For 10 days after an email or phone change, a newly linked bank account cannot receive electronic redemptions without a verification call.

These rules protect clients. Explain them as protection: "Because your address just changed, for the next 15 days we'll need extra verification to mail a check there. That protects you if someone else ever tried to change your address."

Moving between states

An address change to a new state can affect state tax withholding on IRA distributions and the account's state of residence for UTMA accounts. Mention this if the client has scheduled distributions. See State tax withholding.

Communication preferences

Section: Account Maintenance · Page ID: communication-preferences

Which notifications clients can control, how to change them, and which ones are mandatory.

On this page

  • What clients can control
  • Mandatory communications
  • Do-not-contact requests
  • Example
  • Text alerts

What clients can control

At My Account → Preferences, clients choose:

  • Delivery method for documents: paperless or paper. See Paperless delivery.
  • Text alerts for trade confirmations, deposits, and withdrawals.
  • Marketing and educational emails: the newsletter, Library articles, the podcast.
  • Seasonal address for mail.
  • Interested parties for duplicate statements.

Mandatory communications

The following cannot be turned off:

  • Security alerts (password, contact detail, bank, and access changes).
  • Trade confirmations (they can be delivered electronically but not suppressed).
  • Regulatory notices, privacy notices, and prospectuses (deliverable electronically).
  • Tax forms.

Do-not-contact requests

Clients who do not want marketing calls or emails can opt out of all marketing. We do not make sales calls. A client who received an unexpected call from someone claiming to be from Bob's should be warned about impersonation scams. See Phishing and investment scams.

Example

Client: "I get too many emails from you."

Agent: "I can help with that. Marketing emails like our newsletter and podcast announcements can be turned off at My Account → Preferences. Security alerts and document notices will still come, because they protect your account and tell you when statements are ready."

Text alerts

Clients can opt in to text alerts for trades, deposits, and withdrawals. Standard message rates may apply. Texts never include full account numbers.

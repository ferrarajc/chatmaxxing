Address changes: special situations

Section: Account Maintenance · Page ID: address-changes

Seasonal addresses, temporary addresses, returned mail, military addresses, and moves abroad.

On this page

  • Seasonal and temporary addresses
  • Returned mail
  • Military addresses
  • Moving abroad

Seasonal and temporary addresses

Clients who split the year between two homes (for example, summer in Maine, winter in Florida) can add a seasonal address with start and end dates at My Account → Profile. Mail goes to the seasonal address during those dates and reverts automatically. The legal (residential) address does not change, so state tax withholding follows the legal address.

A temporary address for a single month (travel, hospital stay) can be set the same way.

Returned mail

If mail is returned as undeliverable, the account is flagged "address unknown":

  • Further mail is suspended.
  • Checks are not mailed.
  • After a long period of returned mail and no client contact, the account may become subject to state unclaimed property rules. See Dormant accounts and unclaimed property.

When a flagged client contacts us, verify identity and update the address; the flag clears.

Military addresses

APO, FPO, and DPO addresses are accepted as U.S. addresses. They do not restrict purchases. Service members may also keep a stateside legal address.

Moving abroad

A foreign address may restrict new purchases. See Non-U.S. persons and clients living abroad. Route to Account Services.

Multiple accounts

An address change applies to all of the client's accounts where they are the owner. For joint accounts, changing the address changes it for all owners; confirm that is intended, and if the joint owners now live apart, the account can only have one address of record. Each owner's own profile address is separate.

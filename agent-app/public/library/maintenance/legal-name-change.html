Legal name changes

Section: Account Maintenance · Page ID: legal-name-change

How clients change their name after marriage, divorce, or court order, and why it isn't a chat task.

On this page

  • Requirements
  • Process
  • Why it cannot be done in chat
  • Tips for clients

Requirements

A name change must match the client's legal name on file with the Social Security Administration, because we report tax information under name and SSN. The client must provide:

  • A completed Name Change form (from the Forms page), and
  • A copy of the legal document: marriage certificate, divorce decree that restores a former name, or court order.

Some registrations (trusts, custodial accounts) may need additional paperwork.

Process

  1. The client uploads the form and document through a secure message or the document upload page, or mails them.
  2. Account Services updates the name on all of the client's accounts, usually within 3 business days.
  3. The client receives confirmation; their sign-in does not change.

Why it cannot be done in chat

Updating the name requires reviewing a legal document. The Update Contact Information task does not handle names. Explain the steps and send the link to the form.

Tips for clients

  • Update their name with the Social Security Administration first. A mismatch between our records and the SSA can cause a TIN-mismatch notice and backup withholding. See Form W-9 and backup withholding.
  • Linked bank accounts must still be in the client's name; if the bank account name changes too, they may need to re-link it.
  • Beneficiaries the client named are unaffected, but a client changing their name after marriage or divorce often wants to review beneficiaries too. See Reviewing beneficiaries after life events.

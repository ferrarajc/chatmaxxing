Contribution deadlines

Section: IRA Contributions · Page ID: contribution-deadlines

When contributions for each tax year must be made, why extensions don't help, and how to handle last-day contributions.

On this page

  • The deadline
  • SEP-IRAs
  • Last-minute contributions
  • Advising on the deadline

The deadline

IRA contributions for a tax year can be made from January 1 of that year until the federal tax-filing deadline of the following year, normally April 15. Filing an extension does not extend the IRA contribution deadline.

Tax yearContribution window
2025January 1, 2025 – April 15, 2026 (closed)
2026January 1, 2026 – April 15, 2027

If April 15 falls on a weekend or holiday, or the IRS postpones the deadline (for example, for a disaster area), the deadline moves accordingly.

SEP-IRAs

SEP contributions can be made until the business's tax return due date including extensions (for many sole proprietors, October 15 with an extension). See SEP-IRA contributions.

Last-minute contributions

To count for the prior year, a contribution must be received by the deadline and designated for that year:

  • Online or in chat with ACH from a linked bank: the order date counts, even though the bank is debited later.
  • By wire: must arrive by the deadline.
  • By mail: a check postmarked by the deadline is accepted for the prior year if the deposit slip or note designates the prior year and the check arrives within a reasonable time; advise against relying on mail at the last minute.

Advising on the deadline

You may explain the deadline. You may not advise whether to make a contribution.

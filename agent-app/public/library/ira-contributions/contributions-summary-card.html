The IRA contributions summary

Section: IRA Contributions · Page ID: contributions-summary-card

What the contributions summary on account pages and in the agent info panel shows, how it's calculated, and its limitations.

On this page

  • What it shows
  • How it is calculated
  • Limitations
  • Using it in chat

What it shows

For clients with Traditional or Roth IRAs, the contributions summary shows:

  • Contributions made at Bob's Mutual Funds for the tax year, totaled across all of the client's IRAs (because the IRS limit is shared).
  • The client's limit for the year, based on their age at year end (with catch-up if eligible).
  • The remaining room at Bob's.
  • From January to April 15, both the prior and current tax years.

The same summary appears on IRA account pages in the client portal, in your info panel, and is available to the AI assistants, so everyone sees the same numbers.

How it is calculated

It adds every contribution transaction designated for the tax year in the client's Traditional and Roth IRAs. Rollovers, transfers, conversions, and SEP employer contributions are excluded, because they do not count toward the regular limit.

Limitations

  • It includes only contributions at Bob's. Contributions made at other firms also count toward the limit. The summary says so.
  • It does not check income limits for Roth or deductibility.
  • If the client's date of birth is missing, the limit shown omits the catch-up and is marked as estimated.
  • Contributions still pending (for example, a check in the mail) are not included until processed.

Using it in chat

Quote it accurately and add the caveat: "You've contributed $5,000 to your IRAs with us for 2026, out of your $7,500 limit. If you contributed to an IRA anywhere else this year, that counts toward the same limit."

Catch-up contributions

Section: IRA Contributions · Page ID: catch-up-contributions

Who qualifies for the age-50 catch-up, the amounts by year, and how it's applied.

On this page

  • Rule
  • How it's applied
  • Birthday timing
  • Age unknown
  • SEP-IRAs

Rule

Clients who are age 50 or older by the end of the tax year can contribute an additional catch-up amount to their IRAs: $1,100 for 2026 ($1,000 for 2025 and earlier years). The catch-up is now indexed for inflation, so it can rise in future years.

How it's applied

The catch-up simply raises the client's total limit: $8,600 for 2026 instead of $7,500. It is not a separate contribution type; the client does not need to designate it. It applies to Traditional and Roth IRAs together.

Birthday timing

A client born on December 31, 1976 turns 50 on December 31, 2026 and qualifies for the 2026 catch-up, and can make the full $8,600 contribution any time from January 1, 2026 to April 15, 2027.

Age unknown

If the client's date of birth is not on file, the contributions summary shows the base limit without the catch-up and marks the limit as estimated. Ask the client to update their profile.

SEP-IRAs

There is no catch-up for SEP-IRA employer contributions. A SEP-IRA owner can still make regular IRA contributions, including catch-up, to the SEP-IRA or another IRA, subject to the regular limit.

Trade cutoff and NAV pricing

Section: Buying · Page ID: trade-cutoff-nav

The 4:00 PM Eastern cutoff, how weekends and holidays affect pricing, and how to answer "what price will I get?"

  • What counts as "received"

The rule

Mutual funds are priced once per business day at the close of the New York Stock Exchange, normally 4:00 PM Eastern Time.

  • Orders received in good order before 4:00 PM ET on a business day receive that day's NAV.
  • Orders received at or after 4:00 PM ET, or on a weekend or market holiday, receive the next business day's NAV.
  • On early-close days (the day after Thanksgiving and Christmas Eve), the cutoff is 1:00 PM ET. See Holiday calendar 2026.

This is called forward pricing and is required by SEC rules: no one can buy or sell at a price that is already known.

What counts as "received"

  • Online and app: the timestamp when the client clicks Confirm.
  • Chat: the time the agent submits the approved Proposed Action, not when the client first asked. If a client asks at 3:52 PM, tell them the order must be submitted before 4:00 PM to get today's price, and work promptly; do not rush the confirmation steps.
  • Phone: when the representative enters the order.
  • Mail: when the check and instructions arrive at the processing center.
  • Wire: when the wire is received and matched to the account.

Common questions

"What price will I get?"

"Your order will be priced at the closing NAV calculated after 4:00 PM Eastern today, which isn't known until after the close. You'll see the exact price on your confirmation tomorrow."

"Can I buy at today's lower price?"

Only if the order is received before the cutoff. There is no way to lock in a price earlier in the day.

"Can I cancel an order?"

An order can be cancelled before the 4:00 PM ET cutoff of the day it will be priced. After that, it is final; an unwanted purchase must be sold as a separate transaction (which may be subject to the frequent-trading policy). Online orders can be cancelled from the Pending Orders view; chat or phone orders by contacting us before the cutoff.

Time zones

Always convert: 4:00 PM ET is 1:00 PM in California and 10:00 AM in Hawaii (during daylight saving time; 11:00 AM in standard time).

Exchanges

Both sides of an exchange use the same day's NAVs, so the client is never out of the market.

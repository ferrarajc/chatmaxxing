Purchases by check

Section: Buying · Page ID: purchase-by-check

Acceptable checks, how to send them, when they are invested, and the hold on redeeming shares bought by check.

On this page

  • Acceptable checks
  • Not accepted
  • How to send
  • When it is invested
  • Redemption hold

Acceptable checks

  • Personal checks drawn on a U.S. bank in U.S. dollars, payable to Bob's Mutual Funds.
  • Cashier's checks and official bank checks payable to Bob's Mutual Funds.
  • Checks from another financial institution payable to Bob's Mutual Funds for the benefit of the client (for rollovers and transfers).

Not accepted

  • Cash, money orders, traveler's checks, and starter checks (checks without a preprinted name and address).
  • Third-party checks (a check made out to the client and endorsed over to us).
  • Checks drawn on foreign banks.
  • Credit card convenience checks.

Unacceptable checks are returned to the sender.

How to send

Mail to Bob's Mutual Funds, PO Box 2025, Malvern, PA 19355 (or the overnight address). Include the account number on the memo line and a note saying which fund(s) and amounts. For IRAs, the note must say contribution and tax year, rollover, or transfer. Without fund instructions, the money is held as cash in the account.

When it is invested

At the NAV on the business day the check is received in good order at the processing center (before 4:00 PM ET).

Redemption hold

Shares bought by check can be sold immediately, but the proceeds of that sale are not paid out until the check has cleared, up to 7 business days after purchase. This prevents losses from bad checks. Exchanges within the account are allowed during the hold. See Holds on redemption proceeds.

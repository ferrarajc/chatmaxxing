Non-U.S. persons and clients living abroad

Section: Opening Accounts · Page ID: non-us-persons

Who can open an account, and what happens when an existing client moves outside the United States.

On this page

  • Eligibility
  • Clients who move abroad
  • Tax forms
  • Example

Eligibility

We open accounts only for U.S. citizens and resident aliens with a U.S. residential address and a valid SSN or ITIN. We do not open accounts for non-resident aliens.

Clients who move abroad

Existing clients who move outside the United States:

  • Must update their address. A foreign address may restrict the account: depending on the country, purchases (including automatic investments) may be blocked because the funds are not registered for sale there. Existing holdings may be held and sold.
  • U.S. citizens abroad remain subject to U.S. tax reporting. Non-U.S. residents may be subject to different withholding.
  • Military and diplomatic personnel with APO/FPO/DPO addresses are treated as U.S. addresses and are not restricted.

Route clients moving abroad to Account Services for a review of what their account can do. Do not promise that purchases can continue.

Tax forms

U.S. persons certify their status on Form W-9 at account opening. A change to non-resident status requires a Form W-8BEN and is handled by Account Services.

Example

Client: "I'm moving to Portugal next year. Can I keep my account?"

Agent: "Yes, you can keep your account and your existing investments. Depending on your new country, new purchases, including automatic investments, may be restricted. Once you have your new address, our Account Services team will review what your account can do. You'll also want a U.S. bank account for transfers."

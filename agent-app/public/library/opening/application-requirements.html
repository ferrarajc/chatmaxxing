What's needed to apply

Section: Opening Accounts · Page ID: application-requirements

The information and documents an applicant needs, by account type.

On this page

  • Every applicant
  • By account type
  • Funding information
  • Who may not open an account online

Every applicant

  • Full legal name, date of birth, residential street address (a PO Box alone is not accepted as a residential address).
  • Social Security number or Individual Taxpayer Identification Number.
  • U.S. citizenship or residency status.
  • Employment status and employer name (and whether they are affiliated with a brokerage firm or are a senior officer of a public company).
  • Email address and mobile phone number.
  • A trusted contact person (optional, requested). See Trusted contact person.

By account type

AccountAdditional requirements
JointAll of the above for each owner; choice of registration (JTWROS or tenants in common)
CustodialCustodian's information plus the minor's name, date of birth, SSN; state of the UTMA/UGMA
Traditional / Roth IRABeneficiary designations (name, relationship, date of birth; SSN optional but recommended)
SEP-IRAAs an IRA; employer name; whether a SEP plan document is in place
TrustCertification of Trust, trustee IDs, trust taxpayer ID
EstateLetters Testamentary or Administration, death certificate, estate EIN

Funding information

To fund electronically, the applicant links a bank account (routing and account numbers, or instant verification). They can also fund by check, wire, transfer from another firm, or rollover. See Funding a new account.

Who may not open an account online

  • Applicants under 18 (they need a custodial account opened by an adult).
  • Non-U.S. persons without a U.S. taxpayer ID and address. See Non-U.S. persons and clients living abroad.
  • Applicants whose identity cannot be verified automatically (they may be asked for documents).

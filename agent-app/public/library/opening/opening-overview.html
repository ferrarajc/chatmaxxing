Timeline

Most online applications are approved immediately after automated identity verification. A small number need more documentation and take 1–3 business days after the documents arrive. Paper applications take 5–7 business days after receipt.

Minimums and fees

The minimum to open any account is $1, and a client may open an account with $0 and fund it later. There are no account opening fees, maintenance fees, or IRA custodial fees.

Existing clients versus new clients

An existing client with a verified profile can open an additional account in minutes, through the portal or chat, because we already have their identity information. A brand-new client must complete the full online application, including identity verification. Prospects who ask in chat should be sent to Open an Account.

Funding a new account

Is the money invested automatically?

At account opening the client chooses one or more funds and the amount for each. Money that arrives is invested at the NAV on the day it arrives in good order. If the client does not choose a fund, the money is held as uninvested cash in the account until they place a purchase. Remind clients who fund without choosing a fund that cash does not earn a return here.

IRA funding

For an IRA, the client must say whether a deposit is a contribution (and for which tax year), a rollover, or a transfer. Contributions are subject to annual limits; rollovers and transfers are not. See IRA contribution limits and Rollovers and transfers: overview.

Moving money between the client's own accounts

Money can move between two of the client's taxable accounts of the same ownership by journal. Money cannot move from a taxable account into an IRA except as a contribution (subject to the limit), and from an IRA to a taxable account only as a distribution (taxable).

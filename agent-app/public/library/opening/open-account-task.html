Procedure: Open a New Account (chat)

Section: Opening Accounts · Page ID: open-account-task

Chat procedure for opening a Roth IRA, Traditional IRA, SEP-IRA, or taxable account for an existing client.

On this page

  • What the task collects
  • Before you start
  • Checks while the details are collected
  • After submitting
  • Common mistakes

What the task collects

FieldOptions or format
Account typeRoth IRA, Traditional IRA, SEP-IRA, Taxable (individual)
Initial contributionDollar amount; $0 is allowed
Funding sourceBank transfer (ACH), Check, Rollover

Before you start

  • The client must be an existing, authenticated client. New clients use the online application.
  • Joint, custodial, trust, and inherited accounts cannot be opened with this task.

Checks while the details are collected

  • Roth IRA: if the client mentions their income, check it against Roth IRA income limits. If they are above the limit, explain that a direct contribution is not allowed; do not recommend a backdoor strategy.
  • Any IRA with a contribution: check the contribution against the client's remaining limit (see the contributions summary in the info panel) and ask which tax year it is for, if between January 1 and April 15.
  • SEP-IRA: confirm the client is self-employed or their employer has a SEP plan.
  • Rollover funding: the initial amount is an estimate; the rollover itself is started with Procedure: Roll Over From Another Institution (chat) after the account opens.
  • Check funding: give the mailing address and tell the client to write the new account number on the check.

After submitting

  • The new account appears in the client's portfolio, usually immediately.
  • Remind the client to name beneficiaries for an IRA (link: My Account → Beneficiaries) and to choose investments. Money that arrives without fund instructions is held as cash.
  • For ACH funding, the transfer takes 2–3 business days.

Common mistakes

  • Opening a second Roth IRA when the client already has one. This is allowed, but ask whether they meant to contribute to the existing one.
  • Recording a rollover as a contribution, which could create an excess contribution. Be clear about the source of the money.

Opening joint and custodial accounts

Section: Opening Accounts · Page ID: opening-joint-custodial

Steps and requirements specific to joint accounts and custodial accounts for minors.

On this page

  • Joint accounts
  • Custodial accounts
  • Custodial Roth IRA

Joint accounts

  • Both (or all) owners must provide their personal information and pass identity verification.
  • The first owner listed is the primary owner for tax reporting.
  • Choose the registration: JTWROS (default) or tenants in common with percentages. See Joint accounts.
  • The first owner starts the online application and invites the second owner, who receives an email to complete their part and sign. The account opens when both have signed.
  • Each owner creates their own login.

Custodial accounts

  • Opened by an adult custodian for one minor.
  • The custodian provides their own information and the minor's name, date of birth, and SSN. The minor must have an SSN.
  • The state's UTMA or UGMA law applies, usually the minor's state of residence; this determines the age of termination.
  • The custodian may name a successor custodian.
  • Explain that gifts to a custodial account are irrevocable and belong to the child. See Custodial accounts (UTMA/UGMA).

Custodial Roth IRA

A minor with earned income can have a Roth IRA with a parent or guardian as custodian. The custodian opens it through the IRA flow, indicating the owner is a minor. Contributions cannot exceed the minor's earned income for the year. Keep records of the child's earnings (pay stubs, a log of babysitting or yard-work income).

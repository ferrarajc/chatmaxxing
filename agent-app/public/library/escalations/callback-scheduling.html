Scheduling callbacks

Section: Callbacks & Escalations · Page ID: callback-scheduling

How callbacks are scheduled through chat, availability, time zones, and what to confirm.

  • Time zones

How

Switch the column to the callback scope, or the client can schedule from the virtual assistant. The flow:

  1. Opens with why a callback is the right next step (for example, licensed guidance).
  2. Asks for the client's preferred day and time.
  3. Checks availability and resolves the time correctly (callbacks are scheduled in Eastern Time; the server converts times safely, including across daylight saving changes).
  4. Confirms the phone number to call.
  5. Confirms the appointment.

Availability

Callbacks are available during phone service hours: Monday–Friday 8:00 AM–8:00 PM ET and Saturday 9:00 AM–5:00 PM ET, excluding holidays. Licensed professional callbacks may have narrower availability. The flow offers only available slots.

Time zones

Always state the time in the client's time zone and Eastern: "Thursday at 2:00 PM Eastern, which is 11:00 AM your time."

What happens next

A dossier is prepared for the phone agent from the real conversation. See The phone callback console. The client receives a confirmation.

Changes

See Procedure: Cancel or Reschedule Callback (chat).

Example

Agent: "I'd suggest a call with one of our licensed professionals for this. What day and time works for you?"

Client: "Tomorrow after 3."

Agent: "I have 3:30 PM or 4:00 PM Eastern tomorrow. You're in Chicago, so that's 2:30 or 3:00 your time. Which do you prefer?"

Phone number

Confirm the number to call. If the client wants a number not on file, note it for the callback only; changing their profile phone is a separate request.

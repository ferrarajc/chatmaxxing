When an automatic investment doesn't run

Section: Automatic Investing & Withdrawals · Page ID: auto-invest-failures

Why scheduled investments fail or are reversed, and how to fix the cause.

On this page

  • Common causes
  • Diagnosing
  • Talking to the client

Common causes

CauseWhat the client seesFix
The plan is pausedNo investmentResume the plan
The bank returned the debit (insufficient funds)The purchase appears, then is reversed; a $20 returned-payment feeEnsure funds are available; the plan continues next period
The bank account was closedPurchase reversed; the plan keeps failingLink a new bank and update the plan
The change was made fewer than 3 business days before the dateOld amount investedExplain timing; the new amount applies next time
The scheduled day fell on a weekend or holidayThe investment ran the next business dayExplain the rule
IRA contribution limit reachedInvestment rejectedExplain the limit; pause until the next tax year
Frequent-trading blockRare; automatic plans are exemptEscalate if it occurs
Foreign address on fileInvestment blockedSee Non-U.S. persons and clients living abroad

Diagnosing

  1. Check the plan's status and settings.
  2. Check recent transactions for a reversal.
  3. Check the bank link status.
  4. Check the IRA contributions summary for IRAs.

Talking to the client

Explain the cause simply and fix what can be fixed in the chat. If a returned payment caused a fee and the cause was our error (for example, a plan that should have been paused), escalate for a fee reversal. See Fee disputes and adjustments.

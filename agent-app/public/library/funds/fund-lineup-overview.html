The lineup at a glance

Bob's Mutual Funds offers 36 BobsFunds mutual funds in four groups. Every fund has a $1 minimum, no sales loads, no transaction fees, and no redemption fees. Nearly all are index funds that track a published benchmark.

The original six funds

Long-time clients and older documents refer to the original six funds: BF500, BFGR, BFBI, BFIN, BFESG, and BFST. All six are still offered. The lineup expanded to 36 funds; all are available in every account type and through every channel, including chat.

Talking about the lineup

You may describe any fund factually and compare funds on published characteristics. You may not recommend a fund or a mix of funds. See Comparing funds with a client and The investment advice boundary.

What the fund does

Diversified exposure to developed international markets.

Provides cost-effective access to stocks of companies located in developed and emerging markets outside of the United States, spanning more than 40 countries. Ideal for investors seeking geographic diversification beyond U.S. equities.

In plain terms for a client: BobsFunds International (BFIN) is a international blend fund in our International lineup. It is managed to track its benchmark, the FTSE Global All Cap ex US Index, and trades relatively little (turnover of 4%), holds about 8,512 securities, and costs 0.07% a year, which is $7 per year on every $10,000 invested.

BobsFunds International pays distributions semiannual. With dividend reinvestment (DRIP) on, which is the default, each distribution buys additional shares at that day's NAV. With DRIP off, the cash goes to the account's cash balance or the client's bank, depending on their dividend preference. See Dividend reinvestment (DRIP) and Distribution calendar.

Servicing notes

This fund invests outside the United States, so its returns reflect both foreign stock prices and currency movements. When the U.S. dollar strengthens, the dollar value of foreign holdings falls, and vice versa. International funds may pay foreign taxes on their dividends; in a taxable account the client may be able to claim a foreign tax credit (see Foreign tax credit).

International funds in the lineup distribute semiannually (June and December) rather than quarterly. Clients who expect a quarterly dividend should be told this is normal for the fund.

  • Buying, selling, and exchanging BFIN follow the standard rules: orders in good order before 4:00 PM Eastern receive that day's NAV. See Trade cutoff and NAV pricing.
  • There is no purchase fee, redemption fee, or sales load on BFIN. The only cost is the 0.07% expense ratio, which is deducted inside the fund and reflected in the NAV, never billed to the account.
  • BFIN can be held in every account type we offer: individual, joint, custodial, trust, Traditional IRA, Roth IRA, SEP-IRA, and inherited IRA.
  • Automatic investment plans into BFIN require at least $50 per investment. See Automatic investment plans.
  • The prospectus, SAI, and shareholder reports for BFIN are in the fund document library. See The prospectus.

Questions clients ask about BFIN

"Is BFIN a good investment?" / "Should I buy it?"

You cannot answer this. It is a request for a recommendation. Describe the fund factually (objective, risk level, cost, history) and offer a referral to a licensed financial advisor if the client wants guidance. Use the language in Declining advice requests.

"How much does BFIN cost?"

0.07% a year, with no transaction fees. On a $25,000 balance that is about $17.50 a year, taken gradually inside the fund rather than charged to the account.

"Why did BFIN go down?"

Explain the fund's market exposure in neutral terms (for this fund: foreign stock markets and currency movements). If the drop coincides with a distribution date, the NAV falls by the amount distributed; the client has not lost value if they reinvested. See "Why did my fund go down?".

"What's in it?"

Quote the allocation table above and the holdings count (8,512). The client portal's Research page lists top holdings.

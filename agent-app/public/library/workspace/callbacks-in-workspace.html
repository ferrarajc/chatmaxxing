Callbacks from the chat workspace

Section: Agent Workspace & Tools · Page ID: callbacks-in-workspace

How to arrange a phone callback from a chat and what happens after you do.

On this page

  • When to offer a callback
  • How to arrange it
  • What happens next
  • Changing a callback
  • Example

When to offer a callback

Offer a callback when:

  • The client asks for investment advice, which must come from a licensed professional.
  • The request requires a phone conversation with a specialist (complex estate, some trades by phone).
  • The client would rather talk than type, or must leave now.
  • You need time to research and want to give the client a definite follow-up.

How to arrange it

Switch the column's autopilot to the callback scope, or use the scheduling flow yourself. The client chooses a day and time; the system checks availability, converts the time correctly from Eastern Time, and confirms. Clients can also schedule a callback themselves from the virtual assistant.

What happens next

When a callback is scheduled, the system prepares a call dossier for the phone agent: a summary of the client's goal written from the real conversation, research on the question, a guided script, and the transcript that led to the callback. The phone agent sees it in the callback console at the scheduled time. See The phone callback console.

Changing a callback

Clients can cancel or reschedule through chat. See Procedure: Cancel or Reschedule Callback (chat).

Example

Client: "I'd like someone to look at my whole portfolio and tell me if I'm on track."

Agent: "That's a great question for one of our licensed professionals, who can review your full situation. I can schedule a call. What day and time works for you?"

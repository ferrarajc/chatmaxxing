Chat history and transcripts

Section: Agent Workspace & Tools · Page ID: chat-history-transcripts

What clients can see of past chats, how transcripts are saved, and how to handle transcript requests.

On this page

  • What is saved
  • What the client can see
  • Requests for older transcripts
  • Your conduct
  • Example

What is saved

When a chat with a live agent ends, the full transcript is saved with a short recap. Conversations with only the virtual assistant (that never reached an agent) are not saved as transcripts.

What the client can see

  • At the end of a live chat the client sees "Chat ended." and a Download transcript button that saves a plain-text copy (date, agent, duration, timestamped messages).
  • The chat menu shows a history of the client's live-agent chats from the last 3 months, newest first, each with date, start time, duration, and recap. Clicking one opens the read-only transcript.

Requests for older transcripts

If a client needs a transcript older than 3 months, or a certified copy for legal purposes, take the request and route it to Client Records through your team lead. Records requests from attorneys or courts must go to the Legal department; do not provide transcripts to third parties from chat. See Recordkeeping and retention.

Your conduct

Assume every word you type will be read by the client, a quality reviewer, and possibly a regulator. Never type internal opinions about the client.

Example

Client: "Can you send me the transcript of my chat from last month?"

Agent: "You can view and download it any time: open the chat menu (the icon at the top left of the chat window), choose 'Chat history', and select the chat from last month. There's a Download link on the transcript."

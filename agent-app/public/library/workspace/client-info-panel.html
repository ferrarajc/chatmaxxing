The client info panel

Section: Agent Workspace & Tools · Page ID: client-info-panel

What each field in the client info panel means and how to use it during a chat.

On this page

  • When the panel and the client disagree

What it shows

The client info panel at the top of each column summarizes the client's profile:

  • Name and preferred pronouns, if the client has provided them. Use the client's pronouns when you refer to them in after-chat notes or to another agent.
  • Intent label: the AI's short description of why the client is here. Treat it as a starting hypothesis, not a fact; confirm it with the client.
  • Accounts: each account's type (Roth IRA, Traditional IRA, SEP-IRA, Taxable, and so on), its account ID (for example acc-302), and its total value. The total includes uninvested cash; where cash is shown separately (for example "total incl. $877 cash"), the remainder is invested in funds.
  • IRA contributions summary (for clients with IRAs): contributions made at Bob's for the current and, during January to April, the prior tax year, against the client's personal limit. See The IRA contributions summary.
  • Contact details on file: email, phone numbers and whether each is verified, mailing address.

Account values and cash

A client's account value equals their cash plus the market value of their fund holdings. When a client asks "how much cash do I have", quote the cash figure, not the total. When a client funds a purchase "from my account", the available amount is the cash, not the total; to use invested money they must sell or exchange first. See Funding sources for purchases.

When the panel and the client disagree

If the client describes an account or balance that does not match the panel (for example, "my $50,000 IRA" when the panel shows $41,200), do not argue. Values change daily with the market, pending transactions may not be reflected yet, and the client may be remembering a statement value. Explain the difference neutrally and check pending activity. See "My balance looks wrong".

Privacy

The panel shows sensitive information. Never read out a full account number, Social Security number, or full date of birth in chat. Confirm details by asking the client to provide them, not by stating them. See Protecting client information.

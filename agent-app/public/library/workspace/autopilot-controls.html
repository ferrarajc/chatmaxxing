Autopilot controls: pause, resume, exit, send now

Section: Agent Workspace & Tools · Page ID: autopilot-controls

Exactly how the autopilot control bar works so you can intervene in time.

On this page

  • While autopilot is running
  • Pausing
  • Recovery
  • When the client leaves

While autopilot is running

The composer is replaced by a large Pause Autopilot button. The pending reply shows a live countdown to auto-send. In the final 10 seconds the countdown flashes red, which is your cue to read the reply if you have not already.

Clicking elsewhere in the column, or dragging the resize handle on the AI panel to read a longer draft, does not stop autopilot. Only the controls do.

Pausing

Pause freezes the countdown without exiting autopilot. While paused, the pending reply becomes a compact control bar:

  • ‹ › arrows flip between alternative drafts.
  • Send now sends the reply shown immediately.
  • Magic and Change to reshape the reply in place, exactly as on the Suggested reply.
  • Resume ▶ continues the countdown from where it stopped.
  • Exit ends autopilot for that column and returns the normal composer.

Editing the pending reply automatically pauses autopilot, and your edited version is the one that sends.

Recovery

If the AI has a temporary error, autopilot retries once. If it still cannot continue, it stops and posts a note in the column explaining what happened; you can resume once the issue clears. A client message that arrives while you are editing will never cause a duplicate send.

When the client leaves

If the client closes the chat, autopilot stops immediately and the column shows "Client closed the chat." See When the client disconnects.

The Proposed Action card

Section: Agent Workspace & Tools · Page ID: proposed-action-card

How to review, edit, and submit the structured action a task expert prepares.

On this page

  • What appears
  • Your review
  • Submitting
  • If something goes wrong

What appears

When a task expert has collected and confirmed every required detail, a Proposed Action card appears in the AI panel. It lists the task name and each field with the value collected, for example:

FieldValue
AccountRoth IRA (acc-301)
FundBF500
Purchase amount$2,000.00
Funding sourceLinked bank account

Your review

Before you submit, check every field:

  1. Is the value what the client said? Use the locate button (⌖) beside each field to jump to the place in the transcript where it was established. See Evidence highlighting.
  2. Did the client confirm the recap? There should be a clear "yes" after a full recap.
  3. Does the action make sense for this account? For example, an IRA contribution that would exceed the client's remaining limit, a sale larger than the holding, or a purchase funded from cash larger than the cash balance. Check the relevant procedure page.
  4. Is anything missing? Optional details (like tax withholding on a distribution) may matter to the client.

You can edit any field directly on the card. If you change a value, tell the client what you changed and why, and get confirmation.

Submitting

For most tasks, click Submit Action. The action executes, a reference number in the format REF-XXXXXX is generated, and the client receives a green confirmation card describing what was done. Give the client any next steps (for example, when the money will arrive).

For tasks the client must approve themselves, the button reads Send to client. See Actions the client must submit.

If something goes wrong

If the submission fails, the card shows an error. Do not resubmit repeatedly. Check whether the action partially completed (look at the client's recent transactions), then try once more. If it fails again, tell the client you will complete it and escalate to your team lead with the reference details. See Transaction statuses.

Selling shares: overview

Section: Selling · Page ID: redemptions-overview

How a sale (redemption) works, the information it needs, where the proceeds go, and how it differs in IRAs.

  • Lifecycle

Terms

"Selling" and "redeeming" mean the same thing for a mutual fund: the fund buys back the client's shares at that day's NAV. Use whichever word the client uses; default to "sell".

What every sale needs

  1. Account and fund.
  2. Amount: a dollar amount, a number of shares, or "all shares" (a full redemption). See Partial and full redemptions.
  3. Where the proceeds go: stay in the account as cash, go to the linked bank by ACH, by wire, or by check. See Where sale proceeds go.

Lifecycle

  1. The order is priced at the NAV for the trade date (that day if received in good order before 4:00 PM ET).
  2. Proceeds settle T+1 into the account's cash.
  3. If the client asked for the money to be sent out, it leaves the account: ACH arrives in the bank in 2–3 business days, wires the same day if requested before 2:00 PM ET, checks by mail in 5–7 business days.

Ways to sell

Online (Account → Transactions → Sell), mobile app, chat (the Sell Fund Shares task; see Procedure: Sell Fund Shares (chat)), phone, or a written request with a signature (required in some cases; see Medallion signature guarantees).

IRAs are different

Selling inside an IRA does not, by itself, take money out: proceeds stay in the IRA as cash and there is no tax. Taking money out of an IRA is a distribution, with tax withholding and possible penalties. See Selling in IRAs and taking money out.

Costs

No redemption fees and no transaction fees on any BobsFunds fund. Outgoing wires cost $25; overnight check delivery costs $25.

Holds on redemption proceeds

Section: Selling · Page ID: redemption-holds

Why proceeds from recently purchased shares may be held, for how long, and other holds that can affect a sale.

On this page

  • Uncollected funds hold
  • Other holds
  • Talking to clients

Uncollected funds hold

When shares are purchased by ACH or check, the payment takes time to clear. If the client sells those shares before the payment clears, the sale is processed, but the proceeds are held in the account until the payment clears, up to 7 business days after the purchase. This protects the funds and other shareholders from losses on returned payments.

During the hold:

  • The client can exchange into another fund in the same account.
  • The client cannot withdraw the proceeds.
  • Wire purchases are not subject to this hold.

Talking to clients

Explain holds as protection, give the expected release date, and do not speculate about fraud or legal holds. For protective, estate, or legal holds, route to the responsible team. See Protective holds on money movement.

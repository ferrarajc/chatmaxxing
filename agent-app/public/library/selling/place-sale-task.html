Procedure: Sell Fund Shares (chat)

Section: Selling · Page ID: place-sale-task

Step-by-step chat procedure for a sale, the fields collected, and the checks before approval.

On this page

  • What the task collects
  • Procedure
  • Things not to do
  • After submission

What the task collects

FieldOptions or format
AccountOne of the client's accounts (asked if more than one)
Fund to sellA fund the client holds in that account
Amount or sharesA dollar amount, or "full redemption"

Proceeds stay in the account as cash unless the client asks for them to be sent out.

Procedure

  1. Confirm the request: account, fund, amount, and what should happen to the money.
  2. If this is an IRA and the client wants the money out, use Request a Distribution instead. See Selling in IRAs and taking money out.
  3. If the client wants to move the money into another fund in the same account, use Exchange Between Funds. See Procedure: Exchange Between Funds (chat).
  4. Let the task expert collect details. The expert offers only funds the client actually holds in that account.
  5. Check the Proposed Action:

- The fund is held in that account, and the amount is no more than its value. - "All" or "everything" is recorded as a full redemption. - Account and fund match the transcript (use the locate buttons).

  1. Submit. The client receives a green confirmation card.
  2. If the client wants proceeds sent out, explain the delivery and timing (ACH 2–3 business days after settlement, wire, or check). See Where sale proceeds go.

Things not to do

  • Do not ask the client why they are selling. It is their decision, and asking can feel intrusive. Only ask if needed to choose the right task (distribution versus sale) or if you see exploitation red flags.
  • Do not comment on whether selling is wise, even in a falling market. If the client asks, see Talking with clients during market downturns.

After submission

Point out anything that continues: an automatic investment into the fund just sold, for example. Offer to pause or change it.

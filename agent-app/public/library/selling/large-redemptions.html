Large redemptions

Section: Selling · Page ID: large-redemptions

Handling sales of large amounts: verification, delivery, and the fund's right to pay in kind.

On this page

  • Extra care
  • Redemptions in kind
  • Timing
  • Taxes

Extra care

For sales over $250,000, or that would sell most of the client's assets:

  • Re-verify identity even in an authenticated chat, especially if the request comes with recent contact or bank changes. See Verifying client identity.
  • Confirm the purpose if the client volunteers it; be alert for scams ("my new friend needs help", "I'm moving it to a safe account"). See Elder financial exploitation and vulnerable clients.
  • Confirm delivery: wire or ACH to a long-standing linked bank is safest.

Redemptions in kind

The funds reserve the right, described in the prospectus, to pay very large redemptions (over $250,000 or 1% of a fund's assets in a 90-day period) partly in securities instead of cash, to protect remaining shareholders. In practice this is rarely used for individual clients. If asked, explain it factually and refer to the prospectus.

Timing

Large sales are processed like any other; the NAV and settlement rules are the same. ACH is limited to $100,000 per day, so larger amounts should go by wire.

Taxes

A large sale in a taxable account can produce a large capital gain. Do not discourage the sale, but if the client asks about taxes, explain how gains are calculated and suggest they consult a tax professional. See Taxes on sales in taxable accounts.

IRA distributions overview

Section: IRA Distributions · Page ID: ira-distributions-overview

How IRA withdrawals are taxed and reported, the key decisions in every distribution, and which page covers each situation.

What a distribution is

Any money taken out of an IRA and paid to the client (or to a charity, or to anyone other than another retirement account) is a distribution. Distributions are reported to the IRS on Form 1099-R, issued by January 31 of the following year.

Tax treatment by IRA type

IRA typeFederal income tax10% additional tax before 59½

Decisions in every distribution

  1. Amount: gross (before withholding) or net (what the client wants to receive).
  2. Which account and which funds to sell.
  3. Delivery: direct deposit (ACH), check, wire. See Delivery options for distributions.
  4. Federal withholding: default 10% for Traditional and SEP IRAs; client may choose 0%–100%. See Federal tax withholding on IRA distributions.
  5. State withholding: depends on the client's state. See State tax withholding.
  6. Reason (for reporting): normal (59½+), early, early with an exception, disability, death, and so on.

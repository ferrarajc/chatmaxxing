In-kind distributions

An IRA distribution can be made in shares rather than cash, moved into the client's taxable account. The fair market value on the date of distribution is taxable and becomes the cost basis in the taxable account. Clients use this to stay invested.

Special payees

  • Charities (QCDs): see Qualified charitable distributions (QCDs).
  • Third parties (for example, paying a contractor directly): requires a signature-guaranteed request.
  • Another retirement plan: not a distribution if done as a direct rollover or transfer.

RMD starting ages

The table

BornRMD starting age
Before July 1, 194970½ (already taking RMDs)
July 1, 1949 – 195072 (already taking RMDs)
1951 – 195973
1960 or later75

Examples

  • Born in 1953: turned 73 in 2026. First RMD is for 2026, due by April 1, 2027.
  • Born in 1958: turns 73 in 2031. No RMD until then.
  • Born in 1961: turns 75 in 2036.

Still working

The "still working" exception that delays RMDs applies only to an employer plan at the employer the client still works for. It does not apply to IRAs, including SEP-IRAs. A 74-year-old still working must take RMDs from their IRAs.

In chat

Use the client's date of birth from the profile. "Since you were born in 1953, your RMD age is 73, so your first RMD is for 2026. You have until April 1, 2027 to take it, though if you wait, you'll take two RMDs in 2027."

Clients already taking RMDs

Clients born before 1951 have already started RMDs under older rules (70½ or 72). They continue annually; their starting age no longer matters.

The 2033 change

People born in 1960 or later start at 75. The first of them reach 75 in 2035.

Roth IRA owners

Roth IRA owners never have RMDs, whatever their age.

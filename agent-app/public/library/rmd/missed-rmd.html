Missed RMDs and corrections

Section: Required Minimum Distributions · Page ID: missed-rmd

The penalty for a missed or short RMD, how to correct it, and how to request a waiver.

On this page

  • Waiver
  • Correcting

The penalty

If the client does not withdraw the full RMD by the deadline, the shortfall is subject to a 25% excise tax. The tax is reduced to 10% if the client takes the missed amount and files a corrected return within the correction window (generally by the end of the second year after the year of the missed RMD).

Waiver

The IRS may waive the tax if the shortfall was due to reasonable error and the client is taking steps to fix it. The client requests the waiver on Form 5329 with an explanation.

Correcting

  1. Take the missed RMD as soon as possible: a regular distribution request, noted as a make-up RMD for the prior year.
  2. The client files Form 5329 (and requests a waiver if appropriate).

Our 1099-R reports the make-up distribution in the year it is paid; there is no special code. The client explains on their tax return.

If we caused the miss

If the client had automatic RMDs set up and we failed to pay, escalate immediately to Retirement Specialists and your team lead. We will pay the RMD and provide a letter for the client's waiver request. See Correcting errors (as-of trades).

Inherited IRAs

The penalty also applies to missed inherited IRA RMDs. Note that the IRS waived penalties for missed annual RMDs under the 10-year rule for 2021 through 2024; annual RMDs are required from 2025 onward.

In chat

Be calm and constructive. "Missing an RMD can be fixed. The first step is to take the amount now. The IRS also allows you to request a waiver of the penalty on Form 5329. I'd suggest talking with your tax advisor about the form."

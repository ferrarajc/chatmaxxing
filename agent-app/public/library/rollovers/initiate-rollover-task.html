Procedure: Roll Over From Another Institution (chat)

Section: Rollovers & Transfers · Page ID: initiate-rollover-task

Step-by-step chat procedure to start an incoming rollover, the fields collected, and what to tell the client.

On this page

  • What the task collects
  • Procedure
  • What to tell the client

What the task collects

FieldOptions or format
Source institutionThe plan or firm, e.g. "Fidelity 401(k) from my last employer"
Source account typeTraditional 401(k), Roth 401(k), 403(b), Traditional IRA, Other
Estimated amountApproximate dollar amount
Receiving accountThe client's Bob's IRA

Procedure

  1. Confirm the receiving account matches the money type: Traditional 401(k), 403(b), or Traditional IRA → Traditional IRA; Roth 401(k) → Roth IRA. If the client has no suitable IRA, open one first with Procedure: Open a New Account (chat).
  2. Let the task expert collect the details.
  3. Check the Proposed Action, especially the account-type match. A Roth 401(k) rolled into a Traditional IRA is not allowed; pre-tax money into a Roth IRA is a taxable conversion.
  4. For a Traditional IRA from another firm, this is a transfer, which we request from the other firm. Explain that the client may need to complete a Transfer of Assets form or sign electronically. See IRA-to-IRA transfers.
  5. Submit. The task records the expected rollover so the incoming money is matched and coded correctly.
  6. Give the client our receiving details (see Direct rollovers) and explain the next step: they must request the rollover from their plan.

What to tell the client

"I've set up the rollover on our side. Next, contact your former employer's plan and ask for a direct rollover to Bob's Mutual Funds, payable to 'Bob's Mutual Funds FBO [your name]', with your Traditional IRA account number. Once they send it, it typically arrives within 5–15 business days, and you'll see it in your account."

Bonus opportunity

Rollover conversations may show a bonus opportunity badge. Focus on accuracy and clarity; never pressure a client to roll over.

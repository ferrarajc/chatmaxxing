Handling rollover checks

Section: Rollovers & Transfers · Page ID: rollover-checks

What to do with rollover checks made payable to Bob's, to the client, or to the old plan, and how to send them.

On this page

Check payable to Bob's Mutual Funds FBO the client

This is a direct rollover. The client mails the check to us with:

  • Their Bob's IRA account number written on the check (or a note).
  • A note saying "direct rollover" and which account and fund(s) to invest in.

Check payable to the client personally

This is an indirect rollover, with 20% likely withheld. The client can:

  1. Deposit it in their bank and send us a check for the full original amount (making up the withholding) within 60 days, or
  2. Endorse it to us: "Pay to the order of Bob's Mutual Funds FBO [name], account [number]", sign it, and mail it with a note marked "rollover". They should send any make-up amount separately.

See Indirect (60-day) rollovers.

Check payable to the old plan or another firm

We cannot deposit it. The client must contact the issuer to reissue it.

Deadlines

Indirect rollovers must be deposited with us within 60 days of the client receiving the distribution. Allow mail time.

Tracking

Once we receive the check, it appears in the account as a pending rollover and is invested on the day it is processed. See Tracking rollovers and transfers.

Example note to include with a check

"Direct rollover from Acme Corp 401(k). Deposit into Traditional IRA account 4419-8823 for Jane Smith. Invest 100% in BFTM."

If the check is lost

The plan issued the check, so the plan must stop payment and reissue it. If it was an indirect rollover, the 60-day clock still runs from when the client received the original distribution; the IRS may grant relief for lost checks through self-certification.

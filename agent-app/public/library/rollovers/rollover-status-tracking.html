Tracking rollovers and transfers

Section: Rollovers & Transfers · Page ID: rollover-status-tracking

How clients and agents see the status of incoming money, and what to do when it's late.

On this page

  • Where to look
  • Statuses
  • When it's late
  • Communicate realistic timelines

Where to look

  • Client: My Account → Transfer Assets shows each transfer's status. Rollovers appear as pending deposits once recorded.
  • Agent: the client's recent transactions and pending activity.

Statuses

StatusMeaning
RequestedWe have the request (rollover recorded or ACAT submitted)
In review at other firmWaiting for the delivering firm to validate
RejectedThe other firm rejected it; the reason is shown (often a registration mismatch)
In transitAssets or a check are on the way
ReceivedFunds arrived and were deposited
CompleteFunds invested or held as cash per instructions

When it's late

SituationAction
Employer plan rollover not received after 15 business daysClient should confirm with the plan that it was sent, how (check or wire), and the date; ask for a check number or tracking
ACAT not complete after 7 business daysCheck for a rejection; Money Movement Operations can contact the other firm
Check sent to the client by mistakeSee Handling rollover checks
Rejected ACATExplain the reason; usually the client needs a matching account or a statement

Communicate realistic timelines

We control only what happens after the money arrives. Say "typically" and give the client the next checkpoint: "If it hasn't arrived by the 20th, contact the plan to confirm it was sent, and let us know."

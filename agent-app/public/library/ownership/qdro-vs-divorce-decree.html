QDRO versus divorce decree

Section: Ownership Changes & Life Events · Page ID: qdro-vs-divorce-decree

The difference between a QDRO and a divorce decree, and which one each account type needs.

On this page

  • Comparison
  • Special QDRO rule
  • At Bob's
  • Example

Comparison

QDRODivorce decree / separation agreement
Used forEmployer plans: 401(k), 403(b), pensionsIRAs and taxable accounts
Issued byA court, approved by the plan administratorA court
What it doesAssigns part of the plan to an "alternate payee"Divides marital property
Tax effectAlternate payee can roll to their IRA tax-freeIRA transfer incident to divorce is tax-free

Special QDRO rule

An alternate payee spouse receiving a distribution from an employer plan under a QDRO can take it in cash without the 10% early-withdrawal penalty (it is still taxable). Once rolled into an IRA, the IRA penalty rules apply. Mention this neutrally to a client receiving QDRO money who may need cash.

At Bob's

We do not administer employer plans, so we do not process QDROs. We receive rollovers of QDRO distributions into IRAs. We process IRA divisions under divorce decrees.

Example

A client's divorce decree awards her half of her ex-husband's 401(k) and $50,000 from his IRA at Bob's. The 401(k) portion needs a QDRO approved by the plan; once paid, she can roll it into her own IRA at Bob's. The IRA portion is transferred directly under the decree into her IRA at Bob's. Neither is taxable to her at the time.

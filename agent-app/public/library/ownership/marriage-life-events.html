Marriage and other happy life events

Section: Ownership Changes & Life Events · Page ID: marriage-life-events

Account housekeeping clients often want after marriage, a new child, a new job, or retirement.

On this page

  • Marriage
  • New child or grandchild
  • New job
  • Retirement
  • How to raise these
  • Example conversation

Marriage

  • Name change, if any. See Legal name changes.
  • Review beneficiaries. See Reviewing beneficiaries after life events.
  • Consider adding a spouse as joint owner or authorized user. See Registration changes and Authorized users and access levels.
  • Spousal IRA contributions become possible if one spouse has little income. See Spousal IRA contributions.

New child or grandchild

  • Review beneficiaries.
  • Custodial accounts for the child. See Custodial accounts (UTMA/UGMA).

New job

  • Rolling over the old employer's 401(k) is an option. See Rolling over a 401(k).
  • A workplace plan may affect Traditional IRA deductibility. See Traditional IRA deductibility.

Retirement

  • Systematic withdrawals for income. See Systematic withdrawal plans.
  • RMDs approaching. See RMDs: overview.
  • Rollover of workplace plans.

How to raise these

Mention one relevant service, naturally, as a helpful reminder: "Congratulations on the wedding! If you'd like, I can show you how to update your beneficiaries." Never pressure, never advise. See Proactive service and cross-sell.

Example conversation

Client: "I got married last month and changed my last name."

Agent: "Congratulations! For the name change, you'll upload our name change form and a copy of your marriage certificate. And since your Roth IRA lists your sister as beneficiary, would you like to review that too?"

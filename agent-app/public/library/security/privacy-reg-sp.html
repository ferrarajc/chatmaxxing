Protecting client information

Section: Identity, Privacy & Security · Page ID: privacy-reg-sp

Privacy rules for chat: what never to type, how to handle sensitive data the client sends, and our privacy obligations.

Our obligations

Bob's Mutual Funds is required by federal privacy rules (including Regulation S-P) to protect clients' nonpublic personal information: names combined with account numbers, Social Security numbers, balances, transactions, and similar data. Clients receive our privacy notice when they open an account and annually. We do not sell client information.

What never to type in chat

  • A full Social Security number or taxpayer ID.
  • A full account number or bank account number. Use the last four digits or the account type and ID shown in the portal (for example "Roth IRA, acc-301").
  • A full date of birth.
  • Passwords, one-time codes, or security answers. We never ask for a client's password.

When a client sends sensitive data

Clients sometimes paste a full SSN or bank account number into chat. Do not repeat it. Say: "Thank you. For your security, please don't share full account or Social Security numbers in chat; I have what I need." Report it per your team's procedure so the transcript can be redacted.

Sharing information

Share account information only with people entitled to it (see What you can discuss with whom). Never confirm whether a person is a client to someone who is not authorized. "I'm not able to discuss whether someone has an account with us" is the correct answer.

Screens and workspace

Lock your screen whenever you step away. Do not copy client data into personal notes, email, or messaging apps. Use only approved tools.

Privacy questions from clients

Clients can read the privacy notice in the portal footer. Requests to change marketing preferences can be handled at My Account → Preferences. Questions about data deletion or legal privacy rights go to the Privacy Office through your team lead.

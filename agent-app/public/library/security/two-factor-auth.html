Two-factor authentication (2FA)

Section: Identity, Privacy & Security · Page ID: two-factor-auth

How 2FA works on our accounts, enabling and changing methods, and recovering when a client loses their device.

On this page

  • Requirement
  • Methods
  • Changing methods
  • Lost device

Requirement

Two-factor authentication is required for every online account. After entering their password, the client must confirm a one-time code sent by text message or generated by an authenticator app, or approve a sign-in prompt in the mobile app.

Methods

MethodHow it worksNotes
Text messageA 6-digit code is texted to a verified mobile numberMost common; requires a verified phone
Authenticator appA rotating code from an app the client sets up with a QR codeMost secure; works without cell service
Mobile app approvalA push prompt in the Bob's Mutual Funds appRequires the app on a trusted device

Changing methods

Clients can add or change methods at My Account → Security. Through chat, the Update Security Settings task can enable 2FA or remove a trusted device. Because 2FA is required, a request to "disable 2FA" is processed only as replacing one method with another; we do not leave an account without a second factor. Explain this to the client.

Lost device

If a client has lost access to their 2FA device and cannot sign in:

  1. They must call 1-800-BOB-FUND; this cannot be completed in chat because the client cannot authenticate.
  2. They will need the last four digits of their SSN, their account number, and their security question answers.
  3. A 2FA reset takes up to 1 business day.

If the client is already signed in (for example, they have a new phone but the old one still works), they can switch methods themselves at My Account → Security.

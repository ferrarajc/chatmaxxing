Conversion mistakes to prevent

Section: Roth Conversions · Page ID: conversion-mistakes

The errors clients make with conversions and how to help them avoid each, without giving advice.

Common mistakes

MistakeWhat happensHow to help
Expecting to undo it laterConversions can't be recharacterizedState it before submitting
Converting the RMDRMDs can't be converted; an excess contribution resultsConfirm the RMD is taken first
Withholding tax from the conversion under 59½The withheld part is a distribution, possibly penalizedExplain withholding consequences
Expecting to designate the prior yearConversions count for the year processedExplain the calendar-year rule
Forgetting the pro-rata ruleMore of the conversion is taxable than expectedMention the rule exists; refer to a tax advisor
Converting SEP money while contributing the same yearAllowed, but the year-end balance affects the pro-rata calculationRefer to a tax advisor
Forgetting beneficiaries on a new Roth IRAThe Roth IRA has no beneficiaryRemind them to name beneficiaries
Converting from an inherited IRANot allowed for non-spouse beneficiariesExplain; refer to Estate Services

Tone

Present these as things to know, not reasons not to convert. The decision is the client's.

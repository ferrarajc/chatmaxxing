Forms by account type

AccountFormsWhat they report
Taxable (individual, joint, trust, custodial)Consolidated Form 1099 (1099-DIV, 1099-B, 1099-INT if any)Dividends, capital gain distributions, sales and exchanges, foreign tax paid
Traditional, Roth, SEP, inherited IRAForm 1099-R (if there was a distribution)Distributions, conversions, excess removals
Traditional, Roth, SEP, inherited IRAForm 5498Contributions, rollovers, conversions, year-end value, RMD for next year

IRA dividends and trades are not reported each year; nothing is taxable until a distribution.

Where to find forms

My Account → Tax Documents. Forms stay online for 7 years. Paperless clients receive an email when forms are ready; others receive paper by mail by the deadlines. See Tax form calendar.

Not every client gets every form

  • No 1099-DIV if the account received less than $10 of dividends (but the income is still taxable).
  • No 1099-B if there were no sales or exchanges in a taxable account.
  • No 1099-R if there were no IRA distributions.
  • Form 5498 is produced for every IRA with a year-end balance.

Tax season tips

  • Forms can't be produced earlier than their availability date. Clients who file very early may need a corrected return if a form changes. See Corrected tax forms.
  • Clients who say a form is "missing" usually had no reportable activity on that account or are looking at the wrong year.

The advice boundary

Explain what each form shows and where the numbers come from. Do not tell clients how to file, what is deductible, or how much they will owe. See Tax information versus tax advice.

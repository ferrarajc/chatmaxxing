Long-term rates (2025 tax year)

Thresholds are adjusted for inflation each year; 2026 thresholds are slightly higher.

Short-term gains

Gains on shares held one year or less are taxed as ordinary income, at rates up to 37%.

Net investment income tax

An additional 3.8% applies to investment income for clients with modified AGI above $200,000 (single) or $250,000 (married filing jointly).

State taxes

Most states tax capital gains as ordinary income. A few have no income tax.

How to discuss

These are published facts you may share. Do not estimate a client's tax or suggest timing sales for tax purposes.

Holding period examples

  • Shares bought March 3, 2025 and sold March 3, 2026: held exactly one year, which is short-term. Long-term requires more than one year (sold March 4, 2026 or later).
  • Under average cost, the holding period uses the first-in shares for the shares sold, so a mix of long- and short-term may be reported.

Capital gain distributions

Long-term capital gain distributions from a fund are long-term regardless of how long the client has owned the fund.

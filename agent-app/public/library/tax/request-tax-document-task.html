Procedure: Request Tax Document (chat)

Section: Tax Center · Page ID: request-tax-document-task

Step-by-step chat procedure for sending a client a copy of a tax form, and what to check first.

What the task collects

FieldOptions or format
Form type1099-R, 1099-B, 1099-DIV, 5498
Tax yearThe year the client needs

Before you start

  • Check availability: forms are not available before their dates. A 2026 Form 5498 requested in March 2027 does not exist yet. See Tax form calendar.
  • Check the account type: IRAs produce 1099-R and 5498; taxable accounts produce the consolidated 1099 (DIV and B). A client asking for a "1099" for their Roth IRA with no distributions will not have one.
  • Self-service first: the client can download any form from the last 7 years at My Account → Tax Documents in seconds. Offer the link; if they prefer, proceed with the task.

Procedure

  1. Confirm the form and year.
  2. Let the task expert collect them, or choose the task from the menu.
  3. Review the Proposed Action and submit.
  4. Tell the client how they will receive it: posted to Tax Documents and emailed (paperless) or mailed within 5 business days.

Older years

The task offers recent years. For forms older than 7 years, route to Client Records.

Questions about the contents

If the client asks what a number means, see the form pages in this section. If they believe it is wrong, see Corrected tax forms.

Form 1099-DIV

Section: Tax Center · Page ID: form-1099-div

What each box on the 1099-DIV means for our funds, and common client questions.

On this page

  • What it reports
  • Key boxes
  • Common questions

What it reports

Dividends and capital gain distributions paid in a taxable account during the year, whether paid in cash or reinvested.

Key boxes

BoxMeaning
1a Total ordinary dividendsAll dividends, including qualified dividends and short-term capital gain distributions
1b Qualified dividendsThe portion of 1a eligible for lower long-term capital gain rates. See Qualified dividends
2a Total capital gain distributionsLong-term capital gain distributions
3 Nondividend distributionsReturn of capital (reduces cost basis; not currently taxable)
4 Federal income tax withheldBackup withholding, if any. See Form W-9 and backup withholding
5 Section 199A dividendsREIT dividends from BFREI that may qualify for a deduction
7 Foreign tax paidFor international funds. See Foreign tax credit
12 Exempt-interest dividendsNot applicable to our funds

Common questions

"I reinvested everything. Why is this taxable?"

Reinvested distributions are taxable in a taxable account. They also increase cost basis. See Dividend reinvestment (DRIP).

"Why is the bond fund income not qualified?"

Bond interest is never a qualified dividend; it is ordinary income.

"My fund lost value. Why do I have dividends?"

Dividends and the fund's price change are separate. The fund paid out income it earned.

"What about my IRA?"

No 1099-DIV for IRAs. Nothing is taxed until distributions.

Form 5498

Section: Tax Center · Page ID: form-5498

What the 5498 reports, why it arrives in May, and why clients usually don't need it to file.

On this page

  • What it reports
  • Timing
  • Common questions
  • Example
  • RMD boxes

What it reports

For each IRA, for the tax year:

BoxMeaning
1 IRA contributionsTraditional IRA contributions for the year, including those made January–April of the next year
2 Rollover contributionsRollovers received
3 Roth conversion amountAmounts converted into a Roth IRA
4 Recharacterized contributionsRecharacterizations
5 Fair market valueAccount value on December 31
8 SEP contributionsSEP employer contributions
10 Roth IRA contributionsRoth contributions for the year
11–12 RMDWhether an RMD is required for the next year, and the amount

Timing

Mailed by May 31, after the prior-year contribution deadline. Clients can file their tax returns without it; their contribution records are enough. It is informational: the client doesn't file it.

Common questions

"My 5498 shows the wrong year for my contribution."

Contributions are reported for the tax year designated. If the designation was wrong, see Prior-year contributions (January to April).

"Why didn't I get one?"

Every IRA with a balance gets one. If the account was opened and closed in the same year with no contributions, there may be none.

Example

A client made a $7,000 Roth contribution for 2025 on March 15, 2026. It appears in box 10 of the 2025 Form 5498, mailed by May 31, 2026.

RMD boxes

For clients subject to RMDs, boxes 11 and 12 show that an RMD is due for the next year and its amount (or that one is required). This is also how the IRS knows an RMD is due.

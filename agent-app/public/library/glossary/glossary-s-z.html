Glossary S–Z

Section: Glossary · Page ID: glossary-s-z

Definitions of terms beginning S through Z.

On this page

  • S
  • T
  • U–Z

S

SAI

Statement of Additional Information. See Statement of Additional Information (SAI).

SEC

The Securities and Exchange Commission.

SECURE Act / SECURE 2.0

Federal laws (2019, 2022) that changed RMD ages, inherited IRA rules, and more.

SEP-IRA

Simplified Employee Pension IRA for self-employed people and small businesses. See SEP-IRA.

SEPP / 72(t)

Substantially equal periodic payments avoiding the early-withdrawal penalty. See Substantially equal periodic payments (72(t)).

Settlement

The completion of a trade, T+1 for mutual funds. See Settlement and when money is available.

SIPC

Securities Investor Protection Corporation. See SIPC and the safety of client assets.

Specific identification

A cost basis method in which the client chooses which shares to sell.

Spousal IRA

An IRA funded based on a working spouse's income. See Spousal IRA.

Standard deviation

A measure of how widely returns have varied.

Step-up in basis

Resetting cost basis to the date-of-death value for inherited taxable assets. See Inheriting taxable accounts.

Suggested reply

The AI-drafted next message in the agent's support panel. See Suggested replies, Change to, and Magic.

Systematic withdrawal plan (SWP)

Recurring withdrawals on a schedule. See Systematic withdrawal plans.

T

T+1

Settlement one business day after the trade date.

Task expert

An AI specialist that collects the details for one service task. See Task experts and the task menu.

TOD

Transfer on death registration for taxable accounts. See Transfer on death (TOD) for taxable accounts.

Total return

Price change plus reinvested distributions. See Total return versus price change.

Trusted contact person

Someone we may contact about exploitation or if we can't reach the client. See Trusted contact person.

Turnover

The share of a fund's holdings replaced in a year.

U–Z

UTMA / UGMA

Uniform Transfers/Gifts to Minors Acts governing custodial accounts.

Uniform Lifetime Table

The IRS table used for most owners' RMDs. See Uniform Lifetime Table.

Value (style)

Companies that appear inexpensive relative to fundamentals.

Wash sale

A loss disallowed because the same security was bought within 30 days. See The wash-sale rule.

Wire

A same-day bank transfer. See Incoming wires and Outgoing wires.

Wrap-up code

The code classifying a chat's primary topic. See Wrap-up codes.

Yield

Income relative to price. See Yields.

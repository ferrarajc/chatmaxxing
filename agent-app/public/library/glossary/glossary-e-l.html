Glossary E–L

Section: Glossary · Page ID: glossary-e-l

Definitions of terms beginning E through L.

On this page

  • E
  • F
  • G–H
  • I
  • J–L

E

Early withdrawal penalty

The 10% additional tax on IRA distributions before 59½, unless an exception applies. See The 10% early-withdrawal penalty.

Eligible designated beneficiary (EDB)

A beneficiary who may stretch inherited IRA payments over life expectancy. See Eligible designated beneficiaries.

ESG

Environmental, social, and governance criteria used to screen investments; BFESG applies such screens.

Escheatment

Turning dormant property over to a state. See Dormant accounts and unclaimed property.

Evidence highlighting

Transcript highlights showing where each Proposed Action value came from. See Evidence highlighting.

Excess contribution

An IRA contribution above the allowed amount, subject to 6% annual excise tax. See Excess contributions and how to fix them.

Exchange

Moving money from one fund to another in the same account. See Exchanges overview.

Ex-dividend date

The date a fund's NAV drops by a distribution. See Record, ex-dividend, reinvestment, and pay dates.

Expense ratio

A fund's annual operating cost as a percentage of assets. See Expense ratios.

F

FIFO

First in, first out; a cost basis method selling the oldest shares first.

FINRA

The Financial Industry Regulatory Authority, which regulates broker-dealers.

Form 1099-B, 1099-DIV, 1099-R, 5498

Tax forms we issue. See Tax center overview.

Frequent trading policy

Restricts purchases after a round trip within 30 days. See Frequent trading (market timing) policy.

Full redemption

Selling all shares of a fund in an account. See Partial and full redemptions.

G–H

Good order

An order with all information needed to process it.

Growth (style)

Companies with above-average expected earnings growth. See How the lineup is organized.

Holding period

How long shares were held; over one year is long-term.

I

In kind

Moving securities as-is rather than selling for cash.

Index fund

A fund that tracks an index. See Index funds and active funds.

Indirect rollover

A 60-day rollover in which the client receives the money first. See Indirect (60-day) rollovers.

Inherited IRA

An IRA held for a beneficiary after the owner's death. See Inherited IRA.

Intent label / intent summary

The AI-generated description of why a client is chatting, shown at escalation. See Incoming contacts and the alert card.

Interested party

A person who receives duplicate statements. See Duplicate statements and interested parties.

J–L

JTWROS

Joint tenants with rights of survivorship; the survivor inherits automatically. See Joint accounts.

Letters Testamentary / of Administration

Court documents authorizing an executor or administrator. See Documents needed after a death.

Limited access

An authorized-user level allowing trades and payments back to the owner only.

Linked bank account

A verified bank account used for electronic transfers. See Linking bank accounts.

Long-term capital gain

A gain on shares held more than a year, taxed at lower rates. See Capital gains tax rates.

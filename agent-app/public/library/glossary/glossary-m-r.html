Glossary M–R

Section: Glossary · Page ID: glossary-m-r

Definitions of terms beginning M through R.

On this page

  • M
  • N
  • O–P
  • Q
  • R

M

MAGI

Modified adjusted gross income; used for Roth eligibility and deductibility.

Medallion signature guarantee

A stamp from a financial institution guaranteeing a signature. See Medallion signature guarantees.

Micro-deposits

Two small deposits used to verify a bank account. See Bank verification methods.

Mutual fund

A pooled investment owned by its shareholders. See Mutual funds explained.

N

NAV

Net asset value per share; a mutual fund's daily price. See NAV: how fund prices work.

Net investment income tax

A 3.8% tax on investment income above certain income levels.

Noncovered shares

Shares whose cost basis we don't report to the IRS. See Covered and noncovered shares.

O–P

OFAC

The Treasury office that administers sanctions. See Sanctions screening (OFAC).

Paperless

Electronic delivery of documents. See Paperless delivery.

Per stirpes

A designation passing a deceased beneficiary's share to their descendants. See Per stirpes designations.

POA

Power of attorney. See Power of attorney.

Primary beneficiary

A beneficiary who inherits first. See Primary and contingent beneficiaries.

Pro-rata rule

Conversions are taxed in proportion to pre-tax and after-tax money across all IRAs. See How conversions are taxed.

Proposed Action

The structured card a task expert prepares for agent approval. See The Proposed Action card.

Prospectus

A fund's legal disclosure document. See The prospectus.

Q

QCD

Qualified charitable distribution from an IRA at 70½+. See Qualified charitable distributions (QCDs).

QDRO

Qualified domestic relations order dividing an employer plan in divorce. See QDRO versus divorce decree.

Qualified dividend

A dividend taxed at long-term capital gain rates. See Qualified dividends.

Qualified distribution (Roth)

A tax-free Roth withdrawal meeting the age and five-year tests. See Roth IRA distribution rules.

R

Recharacterization

Treating a contribution as made to the other IRA type. See Recharacterizations.

Record date

The date that determines who receives a distribution.

Redemption

Selling fund shares. See Selling shares: overview.

Redemption hold

A hold on proceeds from recently purchased shares until payment clears. See Holds on redemption proceeds.

Reference number

The REF-XXXXXX identifier for actions submitted in chat. See Reference numbers.

Required beginning date

April 1 of the year after reaching RMD age. See Timing the first RMD.

Risk level

The five-step rating on each fund's profile. See Published risk levels.

RMD

Required minimum distribution. See RMDs: overview.

Rollover

Moving retirement money from one plan or IRA to another. See Rollovers and transfers: overview.

Round trip

A purchase followed by a sale of the same fund within 30 days.

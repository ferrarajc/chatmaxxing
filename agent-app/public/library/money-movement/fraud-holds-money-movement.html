Protective holds on money movement

Section: Money Movement · Page ID: fraud-holds-money-movement

When and why we pause a withdrawal for review, what to tell the client, and who can release it.

On this page

  • When holds apply
  • What to tell the client
  • Who releases holds

When holds apply

A withdrawal may be paused for review when:

  • Contact details changed recently, followed by a withdrawal request.
  • A new bank was linked and a large withdrawal requested soon after.
  • The request is unusual for the client (amount, destination, frequency).
  • Exploitation red flags are present. See Elder financial exploitation and vulnerable clients.
  • The Fraud team has an open investigation.

Federal rules allow firms to place a temporary hold on disbursements when financial exploitation of a senior or vulnerable adult is suspected, typically for up to 15 business days, extendable.

What to tell the client

Be honest without revealing fraud indicators: "This withdrawal needs an additional security review before it's released. Our security team will contact you, usually within one business day, at the phone number on file." Do not say "we think it's fraud". Do not promise release by a specific time.

Who releases holds

Only the Fraud and Security team or the Senior Investor Protection team. Chat agents cannot release a hold. If the client is upset, acknowledge their frustration, explain that the review protects them, and escalate to your team lead if needed.

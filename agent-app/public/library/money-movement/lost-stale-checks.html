Lost, stolen, and uncashed checks

Section: Money Movement · Page ID: lost-stale-checks

What to do when a client never received a check, lost it, or has an old uncashed check.

On this page

  • Not received
  • Lost or stolen
  • Uncashed (stale) checks
  • Escheatment
  • Example

Not received

  1. Confirm the date issued and the address it was mailed to.
  2. Allow 10 business days for standard mail before taking action.
  3. After 10 business days, the client can request a stop payment and reissue. See Stop payments.

Lost or stolen

Request a stop payment immediately. Once the stop payment is confirmed, we reissue the check, by ACH if the client prefers (faster and safer). There is no fee for a stop payment and reissue on a check we mailed.

Uncashed (stale) checks

Our checks are void after 180 days. If a client finds an old uncashed check, they should not deposit it. We cancel the old check and reissue the funds. Uncashed IRA distribution checks are still taxable distributions in the year issued; the 1099-R is not changed.

Escheatment

Checks that remain uncashed for several years may be turned over to the state as unclaimed property. See Dormant accounts and unclaimed property.

Example

A client finds a distribution check from 14 months ago in a drawer. It is void after 180 days. We cancel it and reissue the funds by ACH or a new check. The tax reporting for the original year doesn't change, because the distribution was made when the check was issued.

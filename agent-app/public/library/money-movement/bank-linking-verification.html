Bank verification methods

Section: Money Movement · Page ID: bank-linking-verification

Instant verification versus micro-deposits, what the client sees, and how to fix failed verifications.

Instant verification

The client selects their bank and signs in through a secure connection service. We receive confirmation that the account exists and the owner's name matches, then the bank is linked immediately. Their bank credentials are never shared with us.

Instant verification may not be available for small banks and credit unions, or when the bank requires extra security steps. In that case, use micro-deposits.

Micro-deposits

  1. The client enters the routing number and account number (and chooses checking or savings).
  2. Within 1–2 business days, two small deposits (each under $1) appear in their bank account, labeled from Bob's Mutual Funds.
  3. The client enters the two amounts at My Account → Linked Bank Accounts.
  4. The bank is linked, and the deposits are withdrawn.

The amounts must be entered within 14 days. Three wrong attempts lock the verification; the client must start again.

Name matching

The bank account must belong to at least one owner of the Bob's account. If the names do not match (for example, a business account or a relative's account), verification fails. See Linking bank accounts.

Troubleshooting

See "I can't link my bank".

Which method to recommend

Instant verification is fastest. Micro-deposits work for any U.S. bank but take a couple of days. If the client needs to invest right away and instant verification isn't available, a wire or check is faster than waiting for micro-deposits.

Security

Instant verification uses read-only access to confirm the account. We never see or store the client's bank password. Clients who are uncomfortable can always use micro-deposits.

Returned payments and items

Section: Money Movement · Page ID: returned-items

What happens when an ACH, check, or wire is returned, fees, and repeated-return restrictions.

On this page

  • Returned deposits
  • Returned withdrawals
  • Return codes
  • Example
  • Restricted purchases

Returned deposits

When a purchase payment is returned (ACH return, bounced check):

  1. The purchase is reversed.
  2. Any decline in the fund's NAV since the purchase is charged to the account.
  3. A $20 returned-payment fee is charged.
  4. The client is notified by email and mail.

After two returns in 12 months, ACH and check purchases are restricted for 90 days.

Returned withdrawals

When money we send is returned (closed bank account, wrong details):

  1. The funds come back to the client's account as cash (not reinvested).
  2. We notify the client.
  3. Once the client updates their bank, we resend.

No fee is charged for a returned withdrawal, but an outgoing wire fee already charged is not refunded unless the error was ours.

Return codes

Money Movement Operations sees the bank's reason code (for example, "insufficient funds", "account closed", "no account", "authorization revoked"). Share the general reason with the client; they should contact their bank for details.

Example

A client's $1,500 ACH purchase of BFGR on May 6 is returned on May 9 for insufficient funds. BFGR's NAV fell from $341.20 to $335.00 in between. The purchase is reversed, the client is charged the loss on the shares ($1,500 × (1 − 335.00/341.20) ≈ $27.26), plus the $20 fee.

Restricted purchases

After two returns in 12 months, the account can only accept wires and checks drawn on the same bank for 90 days. Explain neutrally.

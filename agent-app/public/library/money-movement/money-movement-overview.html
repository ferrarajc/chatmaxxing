Core rules

  • Money leaves only to the owner (their linked bank or address of record) unless a signature-guaranteed request directs otherwise. See Medallion signature guarantees.
  • Proceeds from shares bought by ACH or check may be held up to 7 business days. See Holds on redemption proceeds.
  • Money from an IRA to the client is a distribution with tax consequences. See IRA distributions overview.
  • Recent contact or bank changes trigger extra verification. See Protective holds on money movement.

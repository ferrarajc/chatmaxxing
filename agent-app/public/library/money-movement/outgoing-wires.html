Outgoing wires

Section: Money Movement · Page ID: outgoing-wires

How clients send money out by wire, the fee, cutoff, minimum, and the verification required.

On this page

  • Rules
  • Steps
  • Verification
  • International wires
  • Wire recall
  • Example timeline
  • Wires from IRAs

Rules

  • Wires go only to a bank account in the owner's name that is linked and verified, unless a signature-guaranteed written request directs otherwise.
  • Fee: $25 per wire, deducted from the proceeds.
  • Minimum: $1,000.
  • Cutoff: requests received before 2:00 PM ET on a business day are sent that day, if the cash is available (settled).
  • No maximum beyond available cash.

Steps

  1. If the money is invested, sell first. Sale proceeds settle T+1; the wire can go out on the settlement day.
  2. Request the wire online (Transactions → Withdraw → Wire), by phone, or through chat as part of a sale or distribution.
  3. The client's bank posts the wire the same day in most cases.

Verification

For a wire over $50,000, or to a bank linked within the last 30 days, a representative confirms the request with the client by phone at a number on file. Explain this in advance so the client is not surprised.

International wires

We do not send wires to foreign banks. See International money movement.

Wire recall

Once sent, a wire is very difficult to recall. Confirm the destination carefully, and be alert for scams that pressure clients to wire money urgently. See Phishing and investment scams.

Example timeline

Monday 11:00 AM ET: the client sells $20,000 of BF500. The sale prices at Monday's NAV and settles Tuesday. The client requests a wire; it is sent Tuesday before 2:00 PM ET and arrives at their bank Tuesday afternoon. $19,975 arrives after the $25 fee.

Wires from IRAs

A wire from an IRA is a distribution. Withholding applies as usual, and the client must request it as a distribution. See Procedure: Request a Distribution (chat).

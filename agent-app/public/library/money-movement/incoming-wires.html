We never change wire instructions by email, text, or phone. A message claiming our instructions changed is fraud.

Timing

Wires received and matched before 4:00 PM ET are credited (and invested if fund instructions are included) the same day. Later wires are credited the next business day.

The wire hasn't arrived

  1. Ask the client for the amount, date sent, and the federal reference number (IMAD/OMAD) or confirmation from their bank.
  2. Check the account's recent activity.
  3. A wire with a mismatched account number or name is held while Money Movement Operations researches it; this can take 1–2 business days.
  4. If the client's bank shows the wire as sent and it has not appeared after one business day, escalate to Money Movement Operations with the reference number.

IRA wires

Wires into an IRA must be designated as a contribution (with year), a rollover, or a transfer. Undesignated IRA wires are held until the client confirms.

Example

A client wires $250,000 from their bank on Tuesday morning for a purchase of BFTM in their taxable account. The wire arrives at 11:40 AM ET with the account number and name, matches, and is invested at Tuesday's NAV. If the wire had arrived at 4:20 PM ET, it would be invested at Wednesday's NAV.

Advance notice for large wires

For wires over $250,000, a quick secure message or chat noting the amount and expected date helps Money Movement Operations match it promptly.

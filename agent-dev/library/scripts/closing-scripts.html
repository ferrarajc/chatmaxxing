Closing a chat

Section: Scripts & Templates · Page ID: closing-scripts

Messages for ending chats, including summaries of what was done and next steps.

On this page

  • Templates
  • Closing after a multi-task chat
  • Closing a difficult chat

Templates

After a completed task: "You're all set: your $2,000 purchase of BF500 is placed (reference REF-48213). It'll be priced at today's close. Is there anything else I can help with?"

Final close: "Thanks for chatting with us today, Maria. Have a great afternoon."

After a referral: "Your call with a licensed professional is set for Thursday at 2:00 PM Eastern. They'll have a summary of today's conversation. Thanks for reaching out!"

Idle client: "It looks like you may have stepped away. I'll close this chat for now, but you can start a new one any time, and you can continue this conversation from the chat menu for the next week."

Closing after a multi-task chat

"Here's a summary of what we did today: your beneficiary on the Roth IRA is updated (REF-48213), your monthly investment into BF500 increased to $300 starting October 15 (REF-48214), and your tax form request is submitted (REF-48215). Anything else I can help with?"

Closing a difficult chat

"Thank you for your patience today. I know this wasn't easy. Estate Services will contact you within two business days, and you can reach us any time in the meantime."

Statement of Additional Information (SAI)

Section: Fund Documents & Performance · Page ID: sai

What the SAI contains and when clients need it.

  • In chat
  • Typical contents

What it is

The Statement of Additional Information is a supplement to the prospectus with more detailed information: the fund's investment policies and restrictions, trustees and officers, portfolio manager compensation and fund ownership, the custodian and auditor, tax matters, and how the NAV is calculated.

Who asks for it

Usually professionals: financial advisors, attorneys, or academically minded clients. It is available on each fund's Research page and by mail on request.

In chat

Send the link. You don't need to explain its contents in detail; refer specific questions about governance or policies to the document.

Typical contents

  • Fund history and organization.
  • Investment policies, permitted investments, and fundamental restrictions (for example, limits on borrowing and concentration).
  • Board of trustees and officers, with their backgrounds and compensation.
  • Portfolio managers' other accounts, compensation structure, and fund ownership ranges.
  • Control persons and principal holders.
  • The investment adviser, distributor, custodian, transfer agent, and independent auditor.
  • Brokerage allocation and soft dollars.
  • Net asset value calculation methodology.
  • Tax status of the fund.
  • Proxy voting policies.

When it's useful

Advisors doing due diligence, clients interested in governance, and questions about specific restrictions ("Can the fund use derivatives?") are answered in the SAI.

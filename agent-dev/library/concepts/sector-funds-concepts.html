Sector funds

Section: Investing Concepts (Educational) · Page ID: sector-funds-concepts

What sector investing is, how sector funds behave, and how to describe them neutrally.

What they are

Sector funds invest in one of the 11 sectors of the economy (technology, health care, financials, consumer discretionary, consumer staples, industrials, energy, materials, communication services, utilities, real estate). Our lineup has a sector fund for each.

Behavior

  • Sectors respond differently to economic conditions: utilities and consumer staples are often called defensive; technology and consumer discretionary are more cyclical.
  • Sector funds can be much more volatile than the broad market. BFENE returned +61% in 2022 and −33.5% in 2020.
  • A broad market fund already holds every sector in proportion to its size.

How investors use them

Typically as a smaller "tilt" alongside a diversified core, not as a whole portfolio. That is a description of common practice, not a recommendation.

In chat

Describe the sector and its characteristics from the fund profile. Do not comment on which sector will do well.

Overlap

A client holding BF500 and BFTEC is already about one-third in technology through BF500 (32.4% IT), plus whatever is in BFTEC. Pointing out overlap factually is fine; telling them it's too much is advice.

What the fund does

Stocks in fast-developing emerging economies.

Offers broad exposure to stocks in emerging-market economies including China, India, Taiwan, and Brazil. Emerging markets carry higher volatility and currency risk but provide access to faster-growing economies and meaningful diversification from U.S. and developed-market equities.

In plain terms for a client: BobsFunds Emerging Markets (BFEMG) is a emerging markets fund in our International lineup. It is managed to track its benchmark, the FTSE Emerging Markets All Cap China A Inclusion Index, and trades relatively little (turnover of 6%), holds about 5,800 securities, and costs 0.08% a year, which is $8 per year on every $10,000 invested.

BobsFunds Emerging Markets pays distributions semiannual. With dividend reinvestment (DRIP) on, which is the default, each distribution buys additional shares at that day's NAV. With DRIP off, the cash goes to the account's cash balance or the client's bank, depending on their dividend preference. See Dividend reinvestment (DRIP) and Distribution calendar.

Servicing notes

This fund invests outside the United States, so its returns reflect both foreign stock prices and currency movements. When the U.S. dollar strengthens, the dollar value of foreign holdings falls, and vice versa. International funds may pay foreign taxes on their dividends; in a taxable account the client may be able to claim a foreign tax credit (see Foreign tax credit).

International funds in the lineup distribute semiannually (June and December) rather than quarterly. Clients who expect a quarterly dividend should be told this is normal for the fund.

  • Buying, selling, and exchanging BFEMG follow the standard rules: orders in good order before 4:00 PM Eastern receive that day's NAV. See Trade cutoff and NAV pricing.
  • There is no purchase fee, redemption fee, or sales load on BFEMG. The only cost is the 0.08% expense ratio, which is deducted inside the fund and reflected in the NAV, never billed to the account.
  • BFEMG can be held in every account type we offer: individual, joint, custodial, trust, Traditional IRA, Roth IRA, SEP-IRA, and inherited IRA.
  • Automatic investment plans into BFEMG require at least $50 per investment. See Automatic investment plans.
  • The prospectus, SAI, and shareholder reports for BFEMG are in the fund document library. See The prospectus.

Questions clients ask about BFEMG

"Is BFEMG a good investment?" / "Should I buy it?"

You cannot answer this. It is a request for a recommendation. Describe the fund factually (objective, risk level, cost, history) and offer a referral to a licensed financial advisor if the client wants guidance. Use the language in Declining advice requests.

"How much does BFEMG cost?"

0.08% a year, with no transaction fees. On a $25,000 balance that is about $20.00 a year, taken gradually inside the fund rather than charged to the account.

"Why did BFEMG go down?"

Explain the fund's market exposure in neutral terms (for this fund: foreign stock markets and currency movements). If the drop coincides with a distribution date, the NAV falls by the amount distributed; the client has not lost value if they reinvested. See "Why did my fund go down?".

"What's in it?"

Quote the allocation table above and the holdings count (5,800). The client portal's Research page lists top holdings.

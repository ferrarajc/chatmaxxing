How fund distributions are taxed

Section: Dividends & Fund Distributions · Page ID: dividend-taxes

The tax treatment of ordinary dividends, qualified dividends, capital gain distributions, and interest from Treasury funds.

TypeHow it's taxed (federal)Where reported

Distributions are taxable whether paid in cash or reinvested.

Treasury fund interest

Interest from U.S. Treasury securities is exempt from state and local income tax in most states. Our Treasury funds (BFST, BFITT, BFLTT) and part of BFBI earn Treasury interest, and we publish the percentage each year so clients can claim the state exemption. See State tax on Treasury fund income.

IRAs

Distributions inside an IRA are not taxed when paid. Traditional IRA withdrawals are taxed later as ordinary income regardless of the source of the money.

What you can say

Explain the categories and where they appear on the 1099-DIV. Do not calculate the client's tax or advise how to report. See Form 1099-DIV and Tax information versus tax advice.

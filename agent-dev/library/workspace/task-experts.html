What a task expert is

A task expert is an AI specialist for one service task. When autopilot identifies a task (or you pick one), the expert asks the client for each required detail, one at a time, checks the answers against the client's profile, and recaps everything for confirmation. When the client confirms, the expert produces a Proposed Action for your approval.

Starting a task manually

If autopilot misidentifies the task, or you want a specific expert, open the task menu (the ✈ icon) in the AI panel and choose the task. Your choice overrides the AI's identification. The expert then begins collecting details.

What experts will not do

Task experts stay on their task. They will not recommend funds, comment on whether a transaction is wise, or answer unrelated questions; they will tell the client you can help with other topics once the task is done. If a client asks for advice mid-task, the expert pauses the task and offers a callback with a licensed professional.

Incoming contacts and the alert card

Section: Agent Workspace & Tools · Page ID: incoming-contacts

How new chats arrive, what the alert card shows, and when to accept or skip.

On this page

  • The alert card
  • Accepting
  • Skipping
  • Continued conversations

The alert card

When a client is routed to you, an alert card appears with:

  • The client's name.
  • The intent summary, a one-line AI description of what the client wants, written from their conversation with the virtual assistant (for example, "Wants to roll over a 401(k) from a former employer into a new IRA").
  • The account types the client holds.
  • A countdown showing how long you have to accept.
  • A bonus opportunity badge, when the contact qualifies for a team incentive (for example, a new-account or rollover conversation).

Accepting

Click Accept to take the contact. It opens in your first free column with the transcript of the client's virtual-assistant conversation already loaded and a suggested greeting drafted. Read the intent and the transcript before sending your first message so the client does not have to repeat themselves. See First-contact checklist.

Skipping

You may skip a contact only when you genuinely cannot take it: all four columns are busy, you are about to go on a scheduled break, or your team lead has asked you to hold. A skipped contact is re-offered to another agent. Skipping to avoid difficult topics is a quality issue.

Continued conversations

If the client chose "Continue this chat" to return to a conversation from the last 7 days, the alert indicates a continued chat. If the client asked for you specifically and you are available, the contact is routed to you first. See Continued chats.

Suggested replies, Change to, and Magic

Section: Agent Workspace & Tools · Page ID: suggested-replies

How to send, edit, browse, and reshape the AI-suggested reply.

On this page

  • Sending and editing
  • Browsing history
  • Change to
  • Magic
  • Feedback loop

Sending and editing

The Suggested reply box shows a draft of your next message. You can:

  • Send it as written. It goes to the client immediately.
  • Edit it in place by clicking into the text, then send your edited version.
  • Ignore it and type your own message in the composer.

After you send any message, a fresh suggestion is written for your next turn.

Browsing history

The suggestion box keeps a history of every suggestion in the conversation. Use the ‹ and › arrows to step through earlier suggestions. A spinner shows while the next one is being written, and a small dot indicates a newer suggestion arrived while you were reading an older one.

Change to

The Change to menu offers a few alternative directions that change the meaning of the reply: ask a clarifying question instead of answering, lead with empathy, give a different answer. Picking one writes a new reply along that direction.

Magic

The Magic menu keeps the meaning and restyles the wording: more concise, more detailed, more casual, more formal, or a custom instruction you type ("mention the April 15 deadline"). Use it to fix tone without retyping.

Feedback loop

The workspace records how each of your messages was produced (sent as suggested, edited, restyled, replaced, or written freehand). This helps improve suggestions over time. It is not used to judge you for editing; editing a suggestion to make it right is exactly what we want.

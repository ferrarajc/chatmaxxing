RMDs: overview

Section: Required Minimum Distributions · Page ID: rmd-overview

What required minimum distributions are, who must take them, the deadlines, and how Bob's Mutual Funds helps.

What an RMD is

Tax-deferred retirement money cannot stay tax-deferred forever. Once an IRA owner reaches their RMD age, they must withdraw at least a minimum amount each year, the required minimum distribution, and pay income tax on it.

Which accounts

AccountRMDs for the owner?
Traditional IRAYes
SEP-IRAYes, even if still working
SIMPLE IRAYes (we don't offer SIMPLE IRAs)
Roth IRANo, during the original owner's lifetime
Inherited IRAs (any type, including Roth)Yes, under beneficiary rules. See Inherited IRA RMDs
Taxable accountsNo

When they start

RMD age depends on birth year: 73 for people born 1951–1959, 75 for people born 1960 or later. See RMD starting ages.

Deadlines

  • First RMD: by April 1 of the year after the client reaches RMD age. See Timing the first RMD.
  • Every later RMD: by December 31 of each year.

The amount

Prior December 31 balance divided by a life-expectancy factor from the IRS Uniform Lifetime Table. See How the RMD is calculated.

What we do

  • Each January we calculate the RMD for every IRA at Bob's that requires one and notify the client.
  • The client's RMD settings can pay it automatically: annually in December, or monthly or quarterly. See Procedure: Update RMD Settings (chat).
  • The retirement account page shows the year's RMD, the amount already distributed, and the remaining amount.

Penalty

Missing an RMD triggers a 25% excise tax on the shortfall, reduced to 10% if corrected promptly. See Missed RMDs and corrections.

Factors continue beyond 100 (the table runs to 120 and over).

How to use it

Use the age the client will be on December 31 of the distribution year, even if they have not yet had their birthday. As a percentage, the RMD at 73 is about 3.8% of the prior year-end balance, at 80 about 5.0%, at 90 about 8.2%.

Other tables

  • Joint and Last Survivor table: for owners whose sole beneficiary is a spouse more than 10 years younger.
  • Single Life Expectancy table: for beneficiaries of inherited IRAs.

Retirement Specialists can provide factors from these tables.

A note on outdated examples

An older client-facing example used a factor of 26.5 for age 67; 26.5 is the factor for age 73. Use this table.

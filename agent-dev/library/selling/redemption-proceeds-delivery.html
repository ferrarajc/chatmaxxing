Where sale proceeds go

Section: Selling · Page ID: redemption-proceeds-delivery

The options for receiving proceeds (cash in account, ACH, wire, check), timing, and the rules for each.

  • Rules

Options

OptionTimingCostNotes
Keep as cash in the accountAvailable T+1FreeCan be reinvested later
ACH to linked bank2–3 business days after the tradeFreeBank must be linked and verified; 10-day rule for newly linked banks
Wire to linked bankSame day if requested before 2:00 PM ET (after the sale settles)$25Minimum $1,000
Check by standard mail5–7 business daysFreeMailed to the address of record
Check by overnight delivery1–2 business days$25Street address required

Rules

  • Proceeds go only to the account owner: their linked bank or their address of record. Payment to a third party, or to an address that is not the address of record, requires a written request with a medallion signature guarantee. See Medallion signature guarantees.
  • Checks are payable to the registered owner(s). For a joint account, the check is payable to all owners.
  • If the address changed in the last 15 days, a check can't be mailed there without a signature guarantee.
  • If the bank was linked in the last 10 days, an electronic payment needs a verification call.

Timing from sale to cash

A sale placed before 4:00 PM ET on Monday prices Monday, settles Tuesday, and an ACH lands in the bank about Wednesday or Thursday. A wire requested Tuesday before 2:00 PM ET arrives Tuesday. Tell clients realistic dates and to allow for bank holidays.

Holds

If the shares were bought recently by ACH or check, proceeds may be held until the purchase payment clears. See Holds on redemption proceeds.

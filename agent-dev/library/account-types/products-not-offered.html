Products and services we don't offer

Section: Account Types · Page ID: products-not-offered

What Bob's Mutual Funds does not offer, and how to respond when a client asks for it.

Not offered

Product or serviceWhat to tell the client
Individual stocks, ETFs, bonds, optionsWe offer BobsFunds mutual funds only. Many of our funds track the same indexes as popular ETFs.
Other companies' mutual fundsWe hold only BobsFunds funds. If a client transfers in other funds, they must be sold at the other firm first. See Incoming ACAT transfers.
Money market fund / cash sweep with interestUninvested cash is held in the account without interest. Our Short-Term Treasury fund (BFST) is a low-risk bond fund, but it is not a money market fund and can lose value.
Bank accounts, debit cards, checkwritingNot offered.
401(k) plan administration, Solo 401(k)Not offered. We accept rollovers from 401(k) plans.
SIMPLE IRANot offered. A SIMPLE IRA can be rolled to a Traditional IRA after the 2-year participation period.
529 education plans, Coverdell ESAsNot offered.
HSAsNot offered.
Annuities, insuranceNot offered.
Margin loans, borrowing against an accountNot offered. IRA assets can never be pledged as collateral.
Managed accounts, financial planningNot offered through service channels; clients who want advice are referred to a licensed professional by callback.
CryptocurrencyNot offered.

How to respond

Be direct and helpful. "We don't offer individual stocks; our accounts hold BobsFunds mutual funds. If you're looking for broad stock market exposure, I can tell you about our index funds." Describe our funds factually; do not suggest that a fund is a good substitute for the client's specific goal (that would be advice).

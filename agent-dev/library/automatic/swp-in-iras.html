Systematic withdrawals from IRAs

Section: Automatic Investing & Withdrawals · Page ID: swp-in-iras

Special considerations for recurring IRA withdrawals: withholding, penalties, RMD interaction, and 72(t) plans.

On this page

  • Withholding
  • Early withdrawals
  • Interaction with RMDs
  • Roth IRAs

Withholding

Each withdrawal from a Traditional or SEP IRA is subject to 10% federal withholding by default. The client can elect any percentage from 0% to 100%. Some states require withholding when federal tax is withheld. See Federal tax withholding on IRA distributions and State tax withholding.

Early withdrawals

For clients under 59½, withdrawals are subject to the 10% additional tax unless an exception applies. A specially structured series of withdrawals, called substantially equal periodic payments (72(t)), can avoid the penalty but has strict rules. Clients asking about 72(t) should be referred to a retirement specialist. See Substantially equal periodic payments (72(t)).

Interaction with RMDs

Withdrawals during the year count toward the client's RMD for that year. If a client has a systematic withdrawal plan and also RMD settings, make sure they understand the total they will receive. If the withdrawals already satisfy the RMD, the RMD service will distribute only any shortfall in December. See RMDs: overview.

Roth IRAs

Systematic withdrawals from a Roth IRA come first from contributions (tax- and penalty-free), then conversions, then earnings. Withholding is not required by default on Roth distributions.

Procedure: Set Up Recurring Distributions (chat)

Section: Automatic Investing & Withdrawals · Page ID: setup-swp-task

Step-by-step chat procedure for a systematic withdrawal plan, the fields collected, and the checks before approval.

On this page

  • What the task collects
  • Procedure
  • What to tell the client

What the task collects

FieldOptions or format
AccountOne of the client's accounts
Amount per withdrawalDollar amount, at least $50
FrequencyMonthly, Quarterly, Annually
Start dateThe first withdrawal date
Delivery methodDirect deposit (ACH), Check by mail

Procedure

  1. Confirm the account and that the client wants a recurring (not one-time) withdrawal. For one-time, use Procedure: Request a Distribution (chat).
  2. For direct deposit, confirm a linked, verified bank account. Newly linked banks (within 10 days) require a verification call before the first payment.
  3. Let the task expert collect the details.
  4. Check the Proposed Action:

- Amount is at least $50 and not more than the account's value. - The start date is at least 3 business days away. - For Traditional and SEP IRAs: ask about federal withholding (default 10%) if the task did not capture it, and note the client's choice. - For clients under 59½ with a Traditional or SEP IRA: mention the 10% early-withdrawal penalty may apply unless an exception does. See The 10% early-withdrawal penalty. - Which funds to sell: proportionally by default; note any instruction otherwise.

  1. Submit. Confirm the schedule in plain words.

What to tell the client

"Starting November 1, $1,500 will be sold from your Traditional IRA each month and deposited to your bank, usually within 2–3 business days. We'll withhold 10% for federal taxes, so you'll receive $1,350 each month."

Procedure: Set Up Automatic Investment (chat)

Section: Automatic Investing & Withdrawals · Page ID: setup-auto-invest-task

Step-by-step chat procedure for creating a recurring investment, the fields collected, and the checks before approval.

On this page

  • What the task collects
  • Procedure
  • What to tell the client
  • If the client wants weekly or every two weeks

What the task collects

FieldOptions or format
AccountOne of the client's accounts (asked if more than one)
FundAny BobsFunds fund
Investment amountDollar amount, at least $50
FrequencyMonthly or Quarterly
Day of month1–28

Procedure

  1. Confirm the client has a linked, verified bank account. Without one, the plan cannot run. See Linking bank accounts.
  2. Let the task expert collect the details.
  3. Check the Proposed Action:

- Amount is at least $50. - Day is between 1 and 28. If the client asks for the 30th or "the last day", explain the 1–28 range and ask them to choose. - For quarterly plans, confirm which months the client expects: quarterly plans run every three months starting with the first scheduled date. - For IRAs: annualize the plan (monthly × 12, or quarterly × 4) plus any other contributions this year. If the plan would exceed the client's limit, say so. See Automatic investing in IRAs. - If the client already has a plan into the same fund, ask whether they want a second plan or to change the existing one.

  1. Submit. The plan is saved immediately; tell the client the first investment date.

What to tell the client

"You're set: $200 into BF500 in your Roth IRA on the 15th of every month, starting October 15. The money comes from your linked bank, and you can change or pause it any time at least three business days before an investment date."

If the client wants weekly or every two weeks

Explain that those frequencies can be set up online at My Account → Automatic Investments, or offer a monthly plan instead.

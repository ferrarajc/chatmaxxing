Accessibility and accommodations

Commitments

We provide equal access to our services. Clients may request accommodations at any time.

Available accommodations

NeedWhat we offer
VisionScreen-reader-compatible portal; large-print and Braille statements on request; documents in accessible formats
HearingChat and secure messaging; relay service calls to 1-800-BOB-FUND
CognitivePatient, plain-language communication; written follow-ups; extra time
MobilityAll services available remotely; forms can be signed electronically

Requesting

Clients can request large-print or Braille documents and accessible formats at My Account → Preferences or by asking any agent. Note the request and route it to Client Services fulfillment.

In chat

Adapt to the client: shorter sentences, one step at a time, confirm understanding. Don't make assumptions; ask what works best.

Example requests

  • "Can I get my statements in large print?" Yes; note the request for fulfillment.
  • "I'm deaf; can I call?" Yes, through any relay service, or keep using chat and secure messages.
  • "Your website doesn't work with my screen reader." Help with the task in chat and log a Technical Issue with details.

Paper fees

Clients with an accessibility accommodation for printed documents are not charged the paper statement fee. See Paper statement fee.

Communication approach

Ask what works best rather than assuming. Some clients with cognitive or memory challenges appreciate a written recap at the end of the chat; offer to send one by secure message.

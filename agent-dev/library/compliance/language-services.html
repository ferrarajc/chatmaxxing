Language services

Section: Compliance & Policies · Page ID: language-services

How we serve clients who prefer a language other than English.

On this page

  • Available
  • In chat
  • Example
  • Documents
  • Interpreters for other languages

Available

  • Phone service in Spanish (select Spanish at the start of the call).
  • Telephone interpretation in over 100 languages through an interpreter service, arranged by phone agents.
  • Some key forms and documents in Spanish.

In chat

Chat is in English. If a client writes in another language:

  1. Respond briefly in English (and in their language if you are certified to communicate in it).
  2. Offer a phone call with Spanish service or an interpreter.
  3. Do not use machine translation for account actions or disclosures.

Agents who are bilingual may support clients in that language only if certified through our language proficiency program.

Example

Client writes in Spanish: "Hola, necesito ayuda con mi cuenta."

Agent (not Spanish-certified): "Hello! I'm happy to help in English here in chat. If you'd prefer Spanish, our phone team can help at 1-800-BOB-FUND (choose Spanish), or I can schedule a callback with a Spanish-speaking representative."

Documents

Some forms (account application, beneficiary designation) are available in Spanish from the Forms page. Statements are in English.

Interpreters for other languages

Phone representatives can add a professional interpreter to a call. Clients should not rely on family members to interpret for account changes when an interpreter is available.

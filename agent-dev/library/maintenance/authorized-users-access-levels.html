Authorized users and access levels

Section: Account Maintenance · Page ID: authorized-users-access-levels

The three access levels, what each allows, and the chat procedure for adding an authorized user (which the owner must approve).

  • Typical uses

Access levels

LevelCan see accountsCan trade and exchangeCan move moneyCan change ownership, beneficiaries, open or close accounts
View-onlyYesNoNoNo
Limited accessYesYesOnly back to the owner's linked bankNo
Full accessYesYesYes, including to outside accountsYes, including closing accounts

Authorized users sign in with their own credentials. They can be added to some or all of the owner's accounts. For joint accounts, all owners must approve.

Typical uses

  • View-only: an accountant, an adult child keeping an eye on a parent's accounts, a financial advisor at another firm.
  • Limited access: a spouse who manages investments.
  • Full access: rarely appropriate; usually someone with legal authority anyway. Mention a power of attorney as an alternative for someone who will manage finances if the owner becomes incapacitated. See Power of attorney.

Chat procedure (Add Authorized Account User)

FieldFormat
Person's full nameFirst and last name
Person's email addressUsed to invite them to create a login
Access levelView-only, Limited access, Full access
  1. The account owner must be the one in the chat. A request from the would-be user ("add me to my mother's account") cannot proceed.
  2. Explain the three levels clearly. The task expert explains them too, but make sure the owner understands what Full access allows.
  3. When the Proposed Action appears, click Send to client. This is a client-submitted task: the owner must approve it in their own chat window. See Actions the client must submit.
  4. When the owner submits, the invited person receives an email to create their login and verify their identity. Access starts once they finish.

Red flags

Be alert if the owner seems pressured, if the new user is someone the owner met recently, or if Full access is requested for a new acquaintance. Ask open questions and escalate if concerned. See Elder financial exploitation and vulnerable clients.

Removing or changing access

The owner can remove an authorized user or change the level at My Account → Account Access at any time. Removal is immediate.

Advice or information? Worked examples

Section: Compliance & Policies · Page ID: advice-examples

Twenty realistic client questions, with compliant answers and the lines to avoid.

Examples

Client asksCompliant responseAvoid
"Which fund should I buy?"Explain the lineup groups; offer a callbackNaming a fund
"Is BFGR riskier than BF500?"Compare risk levels, standard deviation, worst years"Yes, too risky for you"
"Should I sell before it drops more?"Acknowledge concern; explain options; callback"Hold on" or "Sell now"
"Is a Roth better for me?"Explain differences; callback"Yes, go Roth"
"What's the best bond fund?"Explain differences by duration and credit"BFST is safest"
"How much should I keep in cash?"Explain cash isn't invested; callbackAny amount
"What did BF500 return last year?"Quote with date and disclaimerProjecting next year
"Should I max out my IRA?"State the limit and remaining room"Yes, always max out"
"Is now a good time to invest?"Explain DCA and lump-sum concepts neutrallyMarket timing views
"What do most people pick?"Describe what the options are forPopularity as endorsement
"Can you look at my portfolio?"Show holdings and allocation facts; callback for reviewJudging the allocation
"Should I convert to Roth this year?"Explain conversion mechanics and tax effects generallyRecommending
"Is ESG a good investment?"Describe BFESG's screens and historyValue judgments
"Will the market crash?""No one can predict markets"; share historyAny forecast
"Should I rebalance?"Explain rebalancing; calculate to the client's own targetSetting a target
"Is my withdrawal rate too high?"Explain the arithmetic of withdrawals versus returns"Yes, lower it"
"Should I take my RMD now or in December?"Explain both are allowedRecommending timing
"Should I roll over my 401(k)?"Explain options and considerationsRecommending
"Is BFTEC overvalued?"Quote P/E from the profile without judgmentValuation opinions
"What would you do?""I'm not able to say, but a licensed professional can help."Personal opinions

The test

Before sending, ask: does my message tell this client, directly or by implication, what they should do with their investments? If yes, rewrite it.

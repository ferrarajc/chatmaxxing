The lineup

Account typeOwnerTax treatmentContributionsWithdrawals
Individual (taxable)One personDividends and gains taxed each yearUnlimitedAny time, any amount
Joint (taxable)Two or more peopleTaxed to ownersUnlimitedPer registration rules
Custodial (UTMA/UGMA)A minor; managed by a custodianTaxed to the minor (kiddie tax may apply)Irrevocable giftsOnly for the minor's benefit
TrustA trust; managed by trusteesPer the trust's tax statusUnlimitedPer the trust document
Traditional IRAOne personTax-deferred; contributions may be deductibleAnnual limit; earned income requiredTaxed as income; 10% penalty before 59½ unless excepted; RMDs from 73/75
Roth IRAOne personTax-free qualified withdrawalsAnnual limit; income limitsContributions any time tax- and penalty-free; earnings rules apply
SEP-IRAOne person (self-employed or employee of a small business)Like a Traditional IRAEmployer contributions up to 25% of compensationLike a Traditional IRA
Inherited IRAA beneficiaryLike the original IRA typeNoneUnder inherited IRA rules
Estate accountAn estate; managed by the executorEstate tax returnEstate assets onlyTo settle the estate

Minimum to open any account is $1, and no account has a maintenance fee.

How to help a client choose

Choosing an account type is a planning decision. You may explain the differences factually, using the table and the detailed pages, but you may not tell the client which one to open. A client who wants a recommendation should be offered a callback with a licensed professional. See Talking about which account to open.

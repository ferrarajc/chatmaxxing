Forgotten usernames and sign-in problems

Section: Identity, Privacy & Security · Page ID: username-recovery

Helping clients who forget their username or cannot sign in for other reasons.

On this page

  • Forgotten username
  • Other sign-in problems
  • New clients
  • Example

Forgotten username

Most clients sign in with their email address. If the client created a separate username, they can click Forgot Username on the sign-in page and enter their email address and the last four digits of their SSN; the username is emailed to them.

Other sign-in problems

SymptomLikely causeFix
"Incorrect password" repeatedlyWrong password, caps lock, saved old passwordReset password
Locked out5 failed attemptsWait 30 minutes or reset; see Locked accounts
No 2FA codeWrong phone, carrier delay, number changedWait 2 minutes, request a new code; if the number changed, call to update it
Page won't loadBrowser cache, unsupported browser, networkClear cache, try another browser; see Website and app errors
"Account under review" messageProtective holdCall 1-800-BOB-FUND

New clients

Clients who opened an account but have not enrolled for online access can enroll from the sign-in page with their account number, SSN, and date of birth.

Example

Client: "I don't remember my username."

Agent: "No problem. On the sign-in page, click 'Forgot Username' and enter your email address and the last four digits of your Social Security number. We'll email your username to you. Most clients sign in with their email address, so try that first."

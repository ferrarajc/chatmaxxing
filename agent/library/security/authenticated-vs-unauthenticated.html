What you can discuss with whom

Section: Identity, Privacy & Security · Page ID: authenticated-vs-unauthenticated

A matrix of what information you may share with account owners, authorized users at each level, and unverified people.

Common situations

  • "I'm her husband; she asked me to check." Unless he is an authorized user or joint owner, you cannot share anything about her account. Offer to explain how she can add him as an authorized user. See Authorized users and access levels.
  • "My father has dementia and I handle his finances." Ask whether a power of attorney is on file. If not, explain the POA process. See Power of attorney.
  • The owner is in the chat and asks you to talk to their daughter. With the owner present and consenting in the chat, you may discuss the account; note the consent in ACW. Transactions still require the owner's confirmation.

Deceased account holders

When the account owner has died, information may be shared only with the executor, administrator, or named beneficiaries, and only after documentation. See When someone reports a death.

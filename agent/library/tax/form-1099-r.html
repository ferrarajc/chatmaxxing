Form 1099-R

Section: Tax Center · Page ID: form-1099-r

How IRA distributions, conversions, and corrections are reported on the 1099-R, and what the distribution codes mean.

On this page

  • What it reports
  • Key boxes
  • Common codes in box 7
  • Common questions

What it reports

Any distribution from an IRA during the year: withdrawals, RMDs, conversions to Roth, removals of excess contributions, QCDs (shown as normal distributions), and inherited IRA distributions.

Common questions

"I rolled it over. Why did I get a 1099-R?"

An indirect rollover is reported as a distribution; the client reports the rollover on their return, and our 5498 shows the rollover deposit.

"I gave to charity (QCD). Why does the 1099-R show it as taxable?"

QCDs are reported as normal distributions. The client excludes it on their return. See Qualified charitable distributions (QCDs).

"Is my Roth withdrawal taxable?"

Often box 2a is blank because we don't know their basis. They determine it on Form 8606.

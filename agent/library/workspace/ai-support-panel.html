The AI support panel

Section: Agent Workspace & Tools · Page ID: ai-support-panel

The suggested reply, predicted topics, predicted questions, and relevant resources: what each is and how to use it responsibly.

On this page

  • Components
  • How to use it well
  • What the AI cannot do

Components

The AI support panel sits beneath the chat thread in each column and contains:

  • Suggested reply: a draft of your next message, written from the whole conversation and the client's profile. See Suggested replies, Change to, and Magic.
  • Predicted topics: buttons for the topics the client is most likely to need next, drawn from our knowledge base and the page the client was on.
  • Predicted questions: the three questions the client is most likely to ask next. One click puts a question's text into your composer so you can answer it proactively.
  • Relevant resources: up to three client-safe help articles chosen for this conversation. Each has a Send button that posts the link to the client as a formatted message. Sending a resource does not change the suggested reply.
  • Autopilot controls: start, pause, resume, and exit autopilot, and choose its scope. See Autopilot: what it is and when to use it.

How to use it well

  • Read every suggestion before sending. The AI is usually right about tone and often right about content, but it can be wrong about facts. Check account-specific details against the info panel and policy details against this library.
  • Prefer sending resources for depth. A short answer plus a link to the full article is better than a long chat message.
  • Use predicted questions to anticipate. If the client asked about RMDs and the panel predicts "When is my RMD due?", answer it before they ask.

What the AI cannot do

The AI support panel does not know about anything that happened outside the conversation and the profile (for example, a phone call the client made yesterday, or a form in the mail room). It may not know about very recent rule changes. When in doubt, this library is authoritative.

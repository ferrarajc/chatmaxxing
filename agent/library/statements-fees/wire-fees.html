Wire fees

Section: Statements & Fees · Page ID: wire-fees

The outgoing wire fee, when it is charged, and when it may be waived.

On this page

  • The fee
  • Incoming wires
  • Waivers
  • Example
  • Free alternatives

The fee

$25 per outgoing wire, deducted from the wired amount (a $10,000 wire delivers $9,975) unless the client asks for it to be charged separately to cash in the account.

Incoming wires

We do not charge for incoming wires. The client's bank (and any intermediary bank for international wires) may charge.

Waivers

The fee may be waived when:

  • The wire is needed to correct our error.
  • A returned wire was caused by our error.

Waivers require team lead approval. Repeated requests to waive for convenience are not granted; suggest ACH (free) when timing allows.

Example

A client sells $10,000 and requests a wire. The wire delivers $9,975 to their bank. If they want $10,000 to arrive, they can wire $10,025 or ask for the fee to be taken from cash in the account.

Free alternatives

ACH is free and arrives in 2–3 business days. When a client's need isn't urgent, mention it.

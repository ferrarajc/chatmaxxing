Addresses

PurposeAddress
Regular mail (checks, forms, correspondence)Bob's Mutual Funds, PO Box 2025, Malvern, PA 19355
Overnight or courier deliveryBob's Mutual Funds, Client Services Center, 1 Bob's Way, Malvern, PA 19355

Overnight carriers cannot deliver to a PO Box, so always give the street address for overnight or courier shipments.

What to include

  • Checks: payable to "Bob's Mutual Funds". Write the account number on the memo line. Include a note or deposit slip saying which account and fund to invest in; without instructions, the money is held as cash in the account. See Purchases by check.
  • Forms: the completed, signed form. Use the latest version from the portal's Forms page.
  • Documents: certified copies (not originals) of death certificates, court documents, and similar. Originals will not be returned unless requested.

Processing

Mail is processed on the business day it is received at the processing center. Checks received in good order before 4:00 PM ET are invested at that day's NAV. Mail can take several days to arrive; for time-sensitive deposits (such as a last-minute IRA contribution), suggest an electronic transfer instead.

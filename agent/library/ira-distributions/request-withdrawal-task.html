Procedure: Request a Distribution (chat)

Section: IRA Distributions · Page ID: request-withdrawal-task

Step-by-step chat procedure for a one-time withdrawal from any account, the fields collected, and the checks before approval.

On this page

  • What the task collects
  • Procedure
  • Exploitation awareness
  • Script

What the task collects

FieldOptions or format
AccountOne of the client's accounts
AmountDollar amount
Delivery methodDirect deposit (ACH), Check by mail
Tax withholdingFor IRAs: federal withholding percentage (standard is 10%)

Procedure

  1. Confirm the account and amount, and whether the amount is gross or net. See Federal tax withholding on IRA distributions.
  2. Taxable accounts: this is a sale plus a payout. If the client wants a specific fund sold, note it; otherwise shares are sold proportionally. Mention possible capital gains if asked.
  3. Traditional and SEP IRAs:

- Withholding: default 10%; the client may choose 0%–100%. Record the choice. - Age: if under 59½, mention the 10% additional tax may apply. See The 10% early-withdrawal penalty. - RMD: if the client is subject to RMDs, this withdrawal counts toward this year's RMD.

  1. Roth IRAs: mention that contributions come out first, tax-free; if the client asks about earnings, see Roth IRA distribution rules.
  2. Delivery:

- ACH to a linked bank: 2–3 business days after the sale settles. The 10-day rule applies to newly linked banks. - Check: mailed to the address of record, 5–7 business days. The 15-day rule applies after an address change. - Wires are available for $25; if the client wants a wire, note it on the request.

  1. Check the Proposed Action: amount not more than the account value (or the available cash plus holdings), withholding recorded, delivery method recorded.
  2. Submit, and confirm the net amount and expected arrival.

Exploitation awareness

Large or unusual withdrawals, especially from older clients, to new banks, or with a third party directing, deserve a pause and an open question. See Elder financial exploitation and vulnerable clients.

Script

"I've requested a $5,000 withdrawal from your Traditional IRA with 10% federal withholding, so $4,500 will be deposited to your bank ending in 4421, typically within 2–3 business days. You'll receive a 1099-R for this in January."

NAV: how fund prices work

Section: Fund Lineup · Page ID: nav-pricing

How a mutual fund's net asset value is calculated, when it is set, and why it differs from stock and ETF pricing.

On this page

  • What NAV is
  • When it is set
  • Differences from stocks and ETFs
  • Why NAV drops on distribution dates
  • Dollar-based orders
  • Where to find NAV

What NAV is

A mutual fund's price is its net asset value (NAV) per share: the total value of everything the fund owns, minus its liabilities, divided by the number of shares outstanding. Every buyer and seller on a given day transacts at that day's NAV.

When it is set

NAV is calculated once each business day after the New York Stock Exchange closes at 4:00 PM ET, usually published by 6:00 PM ET. Orders received in good order before 4:00 PM ET receive that day's NAV; orders received later receive the next business day's NAV. See Trade cutoff and NAV pricing.

Differences from stocks and ETFs

Stocks and ETFs trade continuously during the day at changing prices. Mutual funds do not: there is no intraday price, no bid-ask spread, and no limit order. A client who asks "what price will I get?" should hear that it will be the NAV calculated at today's close (if before the cutoff), which is not known in advance.

Why NAV drops on distribution dates

When a fund pays a dividend or capital gain distribution, the NAV falls by the amount paid out on the ex-dividend date. The client's total value is unchanged: they have fewer dollars in the share price and the same dollars in cash or reinvested shares. See Record, ex-dividend, reinvestment, and pay dates.

Dollar-based orders

Because the NAV is not known in advance, most clients buy and sell in dollars. The number of shares is calculated at the NAV, to three decimal places. A $1,000 purchase at a $218.40 NAV buys 4.579 shares.

Where to find NAV

The fund's Research page shows the latest NAV and the daily change. Historical NAVs appear on the price history chart. Clients' trade confirmations show the NAV of each transaction.

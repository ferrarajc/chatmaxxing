Removing or replacing a linked bank account

Section: Account Maintenance · Page ID: removing-bank-accounts

What happens when a client removes a bank account, and how to avoid breaking automatic plans.

On this page

  • Before removing
  • Steps
  • Closed bank accounts
  • In chat

Before removing

Check whether the bank account funds anything:

  • Automatic investment plans.
  • Systematic withdrawals or RMD direct deposits.
  • Dividend payments in cash to the bank.

If it does, those plans must be moved to another linked bank first, or they will fail. The portal warns the client before removal.

Steps

  1. Link and verify the new bank account. See Linking bank accounts.
  2. Update each automatic plan to use the new bank (My Account → Automatic Investments; RMD Settings; Dividend Preferences).
  3. Remove the old bank at My Account → Linked Bank Accounts.

Closed bank accounts

If a client closed their bank account without updating us, the next ACH debit or credit will be returned. Returned purchases are reversed and may incur a $20 returned-payment fee; returned redemption proceeds come back to the account as cash and are re-sent once a new bank is set up. See Returned payments and items.

In chat

Removing a bank account is done online by the client. If a client cannot access the portal, Account Services can remove it after verification. Changing banks is a common precursor to fraud; if a client is changing banks and requesting a large redemption in the same chat, re-verify identity.

Email and phone changes

Section: Account Maintenance · Page ID: email-phone-changes

Verification of new email addresses and phone numbers, and what goes to each contact point.

On this page

  • Email
  • Phone
  • The client lost access to their old email or phone
  • Communication preferences

Email

The email address on file is used for:

  • Sign-in (most clients sign in with their email).
  • Password reset links.
  • Security alerts.
  • Statement, confirmation, and tax form notifications for paperless clients.
  • Secure message notifications.

When the email changes, a code is sent to the new address and must be confirmed. Until it is confirmed, the old address remains active. A change notice goes to the old address. If a client receives a change notice they did not request, see Suspected account takeover or unauthorized activity.

Phone

Clients may have several phone numbers (mobile, home, work). The mobile number marked for security is used for 2FA codes. Each number shows a verified or unverified status in the client info panel. A new number must be verified by a one-time code before it can receive 2FA codes.

The client lost access to their old email or phone

If the client is signed in, they can update details themselves and verify the new ones. If they are locked out because codes go to an old phone or reset emails go to a closed email account, they must call 1-800-BOB-FUND to verify their identity by phone. Chat cannot authenticate a locked-out client.

Communication preferences

Email and text notification preferences (which messages to receive by text, for example trade confirmations) are at My Account → Preferences. Security alerts cannot be turned off. See Communication preferences.

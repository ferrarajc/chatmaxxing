Procedure: Update RMD Settings (chat)

Section: Required Minimum Distributions · Page ID: rmd-settings-task

Step-by-step chat procedure for setting how and when a client receives RMDs, with withholding.

On this page

  • What the task collects
  • Procedure
  • Notes

What the task collects

FieldOptions or format
Delivery methodDirect deposit (ACH), Check by mail
FrequencyAnnual (December), Monthly, Quarterly
Tax withholdingFederal percentage, 0% to 99%; default 10%

The account is the client's IRA subject to RMDs (asked if they have more than one).

Procedure

  1. Confirm the client is subject to RMDs: an owner of a Traditional or SEP IRA at RMD age, or an inherited IRA beneficiary. Roth IRA owners have no RMDs.
  2. Let the task expert collect delivery, frequency, and withholding.
  3. Check the Proposed Action:

- Frequency: annual payments occur in December; monthly and quarterly payments divide the remaining RMD over the remaining payments in the year. If the client starts monthly in October, three payments cover the year's RMD. - Withholding: a percentage from 0% to 99%. If the client wants a dollar amount withheld, convert it to a percentage or note it for Retirement Specialists. - Delivery: a linked, verified bank for ACH, or check to the address of record.

  1. Submit. Settings take effect for the next scheduled payment.
  2. Confirm in plain words: "Your RMD will be paid in quarterly installments, deposited to your bank, with 15% federal withholding. This year's remaining RMD is $9,400, so you'll receive two payments of $4,700 before withholding."

Notes

  • If the client has already taken distributions this year, they count toward the RMD; the service pays only the remaining amount.
  • The service calculates the RMD for this IRA only. Clients aggregating across IRAs should see Aggregating RMDs across accounts.
  • For QCDs, see Using QCDs to satisfy RMDs; QCDs are not set up through this task.

Checks we receive

Section: Money Movement · Page ID: checks-received

How deposits by check are handled: acceptable checks, endorsement, IRA designations, and timing.

Acceptable and unacceptable

See Purchases by check for the full list. In short: personal, cashier's, and official checks payable to Bob's Mutual Funds are accepted; cash, money orders, traveler's checks, starter checks, third-party checks, and foreign checks are not.

Rollover and transfer checks

Checks from another financial institution for a rollover or transfer should be payable to "Bob's Mutual Funds FBO [client name]" and include the client's Bob's account number. A rollover check payable to the client personally is an indirect rollover; the client may endorse it to us ("Pay to the order of Bob's Mutual Funds FBO [name], account [number]") and must deposit it within 60 days of receiving the distribution. See Handling rollover checks.

Timing

Checks are credited at the NAV on the business day they are received in good order at the processing center before 4:00 PM ET. Mail takes several days to arrive, so the purchase date is usually several days after the client mails the check.

Redemption hold

Proceeds from shares bought by check can't be withdrawn for up to 7 business days. See Holds on redemption proceeds.

Mobile check deposit

Clients can deposit checks of up to $50,000 in the mobile app by photographing the front and back. Mobile deposits follow the same rules and holds. Write "For mobile deposit to Bob's Mutual Funds only" below the endorsement.

Example deposit note

"Please deposit into account 2847-3311 (Roth IRA), 2026 contribution, invest $2,000 in BF500 and $1,000 in BFBI."

Checks from relatives

A check written by a grandparent directly to Bob's Mutual Funds for a grandchild's custodial account is acceptable, because it is payable to us. A check written to the grandchild and endorsed over to us is a third-party check and is returned.

Automatic investment plans

Section: Automatic Investing & Withdrawals · Page ID: automatic-investing-overview

How recurring purchases work, minimums, frequencies, and what clients can set up online versus in chat.

What it is

An automatic investment plan (also called a systematic investment plan or "auto-invest") buys a fixed dollar amount of a fund on a regular schedule, funded from the client's linked bank account. It is the simplest way to invest consistently, and it applies dollar-cost averaging automatically. See Dollar-cost averaging.

Plan details

SettingOptions
FundAny BobsFunds fund; one fund per plan (a client can have several plans)
AmountMinimum $50 per investment
FrequencyOnline: weekly, every two weeks, monthly, or quarterly. Chat: monthly or quarterly
DayDay of the month 1–28 for monthly and quarterly plans (29–31 are not offered so plans run every month)
FundingA linked, verified bank account
Start date, optional end dateSet by the client

How it runs

On the scheduled day (or the next business day if it falls on a weekend or holiday), the purchase is placed and priced at that day's NAV. The bank is debited 2–3 business days later. Automatic investments appear on statements rather than in individual confirmations unless the client opts in.

Changes and cancellations

Changes, pauses, and cancellations must be made at least 3 business days before the next scheduled date to affect that investment. See Procedure: Modify an automatic investment and Procedure: Pause or resume an automatic investment.

IRAs

Automatic investments into an IRA are contributions for the year in which they are made, counted toward the annual limit. See Automatic investing in IRAs.

In chat

Three tasks cover automatic investing: Set Up Automatic Investment (Procedure: Set Up Automatic Investment (chat)), Modify Auto-Invest Schedule (Procedure: Modify an automatic investment), and Pause or Resume Auto-Invest (Procedure: Pause or resume an automatic investment). Weekly and every-two-weeks plans must be set up online.
